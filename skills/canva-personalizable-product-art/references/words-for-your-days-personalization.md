## Field contract

Use the verified design-specific limits above. In the shared rules below, “optional text” means Love’s corner or Faithful’s beneath-leaf initials; retain other designs’ own confirmed contracts.

- **Full name: required.** Capture exact requested spelling, case, punctuation and script; confirm missing/ambiguous input before production. Keep it the design's protagonist in the established type hierarchy. Optional-text font choice does not change the full-name font. Do not invent a universal name-length limit; test the real name against this SKU's usable print area.
- **Optional text: explicit decision required.** Love accepts customer-selected 1–2 corner characters, not necessarily initials; Faithful accepts 1–3 customer-chosen letters beneath the leaf. Preserve their actual characters rather than deriving initials from the full name, forcing uppercase or substituting text. Treat a user-perceived character appropriately for its script; do not let a technical code-point count truncate a composed character.
- **PRINT path:** record text within that product’s confirmed character/letter limit and chosen current verified optional-text style (use A/B/C only on the historical branch where actually mapped). Unclear text, more than the approved character count, missing style or unsupported glyphs remain unresolved; obtain the needed clarification before production.
- **NO PRINT path:** require the explicit choice. Omit the entire optional-text group, including its text and any group-specific mark/placeholder; keep the required full name and approved fixed design. For Faithful, the leaf is fixed artwork and remains printed. Corner font is not applicable on this path.
- **Blank is UNCONFIRMED.** A blank corner field is neither NO PRINT nor permission to derive initials. Hold only the dependent production step and resolve it; do not silently choose a branch.

Minimal order/proof record: exact full name; corner state PRINT / NO PRINT / UNCONFIRMED; exact corner text if PRINT; selected corner style if PRINT; verified font/glyph support; identified proof/version; unresolved items. This is a draft workflow until the actual product and commerce settings are verified and separately authorized.
