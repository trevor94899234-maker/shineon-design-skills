# Product-preserving thumbnail rules

Use these rules whenever the user asks for an AI-generated Etsy thumbnail or mockup for a supplied journal image.

## Product lock

Treat the uploaded journal as the exact hero product. Preserve its material, text, colors, artwork, layout, name, Bible verse, shape, size, proportions, edges, spine, and every original design element.

Only change the surrounding scene, background, props, lighting, camera composition, and lifestyle context. Do not recreate, redraw, repaint, redesign, reinterpret, stylize, destructively crop, stretch or replace the product artwork or construction.

Use the exact local `etsy-listing-image-studio` as the controlling thumbnail generation/QA workflow, defaulting to ChatGPT web image creation through the in-app browser. POD Image Prompts supplies optional role/prompt/source support, not a second generator. Follow Studio's complete-output method, including all requested text and branding with exact product/MG assets as inputs; the owner’s 16 October instruction (`Sentinel_521497cbb0e8819181865e1601f469a5`) supersedes earlier thumbnail-compositing permissions, so historical transforms/masks remain evidence only and cannot restore Canva assembly or later overlays, while original product-art editing remains separately scoped and source/brand accuracy, source lineage and physical-integration QA still apply without a pixel-identity guarantee.

## Finish accuracy

When the product record says the cover is printed or UV-printed leatherette, keep the artwork as flat printed ink. Do not describe or render it as engraved, embossed, debossed, burned, carved, foil-stamped, raised, metallic, or another finish that is not confirmed.

## Overlay and evidence

- Use short, readable overlay text that accurately matches the listing's buyer angle and title meaning.
- Keep the overlay outside the journal cover area; never cover the original text, name, verse, artwork, or layout.
- Show a varied, buyer-helpful image set: hero product, personalization, inside pages, specifications, how to personalize, use/occasion, and trust details where appropriate.
- Verify the final image against the original product before the owner uses it. An AI image that changes the product is a failed draft, not a usable asset.

## Scope, dimensions and release

Keep the current listing angle and image-set identity fixed. Original year-round/anniversary sets, seasonal/newlywed variants and original Q4 artwork are separate deliverables, even when they use the same product. Match scene, copy and imagery to the actual title/tags/description for that set; do not silently replace its buyer intent.

Keep source-native pixels, product print canvas, measured export and listing-thumbnail size distinct. Confirm the current product specification and units; do not stretch print artwork into a square thumbnail or infer supplier bleed/safe areas from pixel dimensions.

Before using a generated/composited hero as a first listing photo, read `etsy-pod-image-prompts/references/listing-image-readiness.md` as supporting first-image source guidance and recheck [Etsy's image requirements](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005/). Personalized first-image source readiness is separate from aesthetic approval, product fidelity and additional-image delivery. Preserve a source HOLD rather than implying that an approved mockup is a real product photograph or permission to publish.

## Accept the set by function, not count

Before calling a coordinated image set complete for its brief, use `etsy-pod-image-prompts/references/listing-function-coverage.md`. Keep its active-brief function-to-file evidence map alongside the product lock. For the current MyGiftStyle journal framework, cover all nine required buyer functions; do not turn a proposed twenty-image allocation into a universal rule. Scene/gift abundance, a name-only label or a page-count sentence cannot silently stand in for missing ordering instructions, size evidence, actual interior imagery or grounded processing information. Preserve verified facts without inventing the missing proof.

Report file delivery, technical/visual QA, factual truth and functional coverage separately. A later audit may find partial function coverage in an already-delivered set; preserve the real delivery history and earlier scoped approvals while reopening the affected functional gate. A category-review question does not authorize new generation, reordering or listing publication.
