# Historical source-preserving gift imagery

Historical reference, not an active thumbnail method. The owner’s 16 October correction supersedes earlier thumbnail compositing permissions: generate each complete thumbnail, including text/branding/light, with the built-in tool; no Canva assembly, cutouts or post-generation overlays. Do not execute the steps below or revive an old authorization for current thumbnails. They are retained to explain earlier artifacts and reusable fidelity/physics QA, not to provide a fallback after failure. Original product-design editing remains separately authorized through the product-art workflow. See [method change record](thumbnail-method-change-2026-10-16.md).

## Inputs and authority

- Exact product master and confirmed specification, including manufacturing method, fixed wording/artwork and the permitted personalization field. Keep unsupported interiors, backs, dimensions and packaging unseen.
- An approved appearance reference for light, composition, typography and gift emotion only. Do not import its product facts, approximate logo or dimensions.
- The original required brand asset, inspected visually and checked against verified provenance. Preserve its relative geometry, colors and original lettering.
- One buyer question, a coherent scene/action, exact overlay copy and room for branding and any illustrative-scene/prop disclosure.

## Create a scene that can accept the exact product

For the authorized compositing route, generate a plausible supported or true overhead product plane compatible with the source geometry. A plain placeholder journal with the correct silhouette can avoid repeated artwork drift. When feasible, omit a protruding placeholder ribbon so the original ribbon can be inserted once. Preserve authentic human contact, lighting and depth; a scene with hands only behind or on blank edges is easier to integrate accurately. Never let a placeholder become the delivered product.

Use varied buyer purposes and genuine actions across a set. Avoid filling a count with repeated crops or background changes. Do not manufacture customer proof, packaging, services, options or product construction to make a scene interesting. Do not rely on a tiny footer to correct a misleading scene or an unsupported product claim.

## Composite reversibly

1. Read and preserve the original RGB product pixels. Start from a verified product matte; clean background contamination only at its perimeter. Preserve source artwork and texture.
2. Identify the actual product plane corners in each scene. Record source and destination coordinates and the destination-to-source perspective transform. Do not reuse another scene’s corners. Work directly into the target canvas to avoid avoidable repeated resampling.
3. Preserve foreground fingers and other genuine scene occlusions with deliberate masks. Color thresholds alone can remove shaded skin or mistake warm background for skin; inspect full composition and close crops. A jagged cover cut-out can be a geometry error, not merely a mask error. Repair the plane fit before adding more masking.
4. Carry only accessories visible in the authoritative source. For a journal with an elastic and ribbon, preserve those originals. If an obsolete generated ribbon remains, remove only that area using compatible same-scene background and inspect the repair. Require one continuous source ribbon, within the frame with clear space. Test the source-mask projection on a canvas larger than the export before cropping: a mask that touches the last output row can conceal a clipped antialiased tail. Do not invent a new ribbon or hide a duplicate under a footer.
5. Insert the verified original logo using proportional scaling and ordinary alpha compositing. Cropping fully transparent padding is acceptable. Do not retype the wordmark, redraw the monogram/star, or alter colors. Keep branding clear of the product; choose a clean scene area with adequate contrast.
6. Keep external text outside the cover and clear of props, hands and busy edges; reserve actual negative space for the whole text block. Preserve exact marketing copy. Do not retype the product cover. Save the native scene, source masks, hand mask, transform parameters, corrected output and version history.

## Artifact classification

Keep the source photograph, generated scene and composite distinct. Inserting verified product pixels into a generated scene does not establish that the complete export is a real finished-product photograph. Apply the first-image/source gate in listing-image-readiness.md independently from compositing QA.

## Verification gates

Compare opaque product pixels to a fresh warp from the untouched master. Record the count and equality result. Account honestly for antialiased boundaries, foreground occlusion and any separately authorized light adaptation; do not call a resampled image byte-identical to the original source file.

Separately inspect natural integration: source-correct pixels can still look flat, float above the scene, cover a fingertip, leave a bright matte fringe, misalign the closed page block or duplicate a ribbon. Repair these before delivery. Do not repaint correct art to disguise a compositing defect.

Open the actual final files. Verify dimensions and embedded color profile, inspect required logo and text, and judge actual 150/300 px previews. Do not count a prompt’s requested resolution as measured output. Distinguish a derived 2000-pixel export from native generation. Record independent QA and owner direction acceptance separately. If the master cannot support a truthful view, identify the specific missing source asset rather than fabricating that view.

## Physical support is a hard gate

Verify the body, contact shadow, support and ribbon share a physically possible arrangement. An upright book resting on a tabletop cannot carry a straight coplanar source ribbon through that tabletop. Exact-source pixels do not prove physical placement. Use a supported hand-held view or a true overhead flat lay when a single-plane source cannot reproduce a bend correctly. Inspect both full composition and the body/ribbon contact region. Supersede prior PASS records and exclude held versions from delivery manifests when a later review finds this defect.

If support remains ambiguous after a full-frame and contact-region check, hold the candidate and choose a clearer scene. When one failure exposes a repeatable pipeline assumption, audit the remaining set for that failure before packaging. Bind each verdict to the final file hash; any later correction requires a refreshed check of the affected criteria.
