# Reference-led product geometry

Use this method for this owner's default ChatGPT web generation route through the in-app browser, and for proportion drift under an explicitly approved alternate route. It supports the existing brief, copy, source, budget and acceptance gates. It does not provide a hard geometric lock or a model-version selector.

The shared source/route rules apply across products. The flat-cover camera, silhouette and apparent-ratio procedures below are **book/journal procedures**. Mats use [mat-floor-plane-profile.md](mat-floor-plane-profile.md); ornaments use [ornament-thumbnail-profile.md](ornament-thumbnail-profile.md). Do not force floor products into a camera-perpendicular plane or apply closed-cover measurements to every category.

## Prepare the same-product inputs

- Inspect the actual source pixels. A complete near-front supplier mockup can anchor silhouette and cover placement; a clear same-product flatlay can supplement material, ribbon or closure details. Keep different reference roles explicit and remove contradictory angle instructions.
- Use the original artwork for exact printed words, typography and decoration. Its print-area aspect ratio is not the physical cover ratio. A square supplier photograph likewise says nothing about a square product.
- Use the approved original logo as the brand source. A generated candidate can inform visual direction when the owner accepts it, but must not silently replace original product, artwork or brand assets.
- Obtain physical width and height from the applicable source or SKU-matched owner confirmation. Keep unknowns TBC and block only dependent rows. Retain source versions and hashes.

## Use the actual authorized generation route

Apply the standing route preference in SKILL.md without requesting it again: ChatGPT web's image-creation mode through the in-app browser, unless the owner explicitly overrides it. Verify the actual page, account access and image-creation control; use the supplied references through the documented upload flow. If unavailable, report the blocker rather than silently switching generators. For a separately selected exact model, verify current official availability and controls exposed in this session. A workflow called Image2.5, text saying a model name, or a product announcement is not a per-call backend model receipt. Record the service name separately from the exact backend model when the latter is not exposed.

Keep the approved whole-image method: product, scene, exact overlay and branding together when the brief requires one-pass output. Do not silently switch to cutouts, pasted products, post-added text/logo, upscaling or a paid API. Reuse current owner authority and limits; a new route does not grant extra calls or spending.

For a **book/journal flat-cover diagnostic view**, request the product plane perpendicular to the camera: true overhead for a book lying flat, or a supported near-front view. This is not a universal lifestyle camera rule. State W/H explicitly, distinguish product shape from canvas shape, keep all face corners clear, and arrange people and props around the correct silhouette. Uniform scaling preserves proportions; squeezing artwork to fit does not. An example such as height800/width546 can explain a 5.8/8.5 ratio, but it is a prompt example, not a tool setting.

Generate the authorized pilot first, inspect its real file, then continue the approved roles in small reviewable groups when supported by that evidence. Keep every role's buyer function and exact copy; a common overhead angle does not excuse near-duplicate coverage. On a row-specific defect, retain that row's failure and continue only independent authorized work. A demonstrated shared failure requires diagnosis and a materially changed supported input/control/method before repeating dependent generation; do not keep spending on unchanged attempts.

## Optional silhouette and role-layout reference

After numeric instructions still produce broad or short products, a synthetic silhouette containing no source-photo pixels, derived from sourced physical W/H, can be a materially changed visual input when the approved generator accepts reference images. Distinguish its product box from the final canvas ratio. For example, a512x750 closed-cover box approximates a5.8x8.5 product; these are case values, not universal template dimensions.

The diagram may include existing exact outside-copy, clear text/product/brand zones and labeled placeholders. It must contain no pasted product-photo pixels or substitute artwork/logo. Keep the same-product original, original print design and original logo as separate sources. Assign each reference an explicit role and resolve conflicting view instructions. For a role-specific layout, use that role's layout only, rather than conditioning on every layout in the batch. A format-only source normalization may retain identical decoded pixels; record and verify that identity rather than treating it as a new independent source.

Generate the whole scene once within the existing authorization and attempt budget. Instruct the generator to omit all diagram guides, colors, placeholder fill and labels from the returned whole image; do not remove them by later editing. Preserve the exact approved copy and original product/brand appearance. The diagram does not authorize post-pasting, stretching, upscaling or extra calls. Inspect the returned real book, not the diagram's known ratio, then apply all existing text, artwork, hand, role and owner gates. A correct diagram or one SCREEN_PASS does not prove batch stability.

Use only a source-supported product view. When the owner supplies an interior, assign that source to the dependent open-page row and keep its existing buyer function/copy. Do not apply the closed-cover W/H to the overall open spread or invent physical open dimensions. Compare visible ruling, binding, cover edges and ribbon against the interior source and keep unknown construction TBC; an open-view source review is not a closed-cover ratio PASS.

## Reusable generation request

Fill this from the approved row; it is a prompt scaffold, not permission for a new generation or a guarantee of fidelity. Retain original source references for each request, including follow-ups in the same conversation. An earlier AI image may guide atmosphere only, not become product evidence.

```text
Create ONE complete listing image for role [ID / buyer question].
Product reference [file]: same SKU's physical appearance and supported view.
Artwork [file]: exact printed cover text, layout and decoration.
Brand [file]: original logo. Interior [file, only for an open-page scene].
Geometry guide [file, if used]: sourced product W/H [W/H], not canvas ratio.
Role layout [file, if used]: composition and outside-copy/brand zones only.
Canvas: [approved aspect and requested native size].
Closed product view: [supported overhead / near-front view]; keep face corners
visible and the sourced product proportions. Do not squeeze the cover art.
Scene: [buyer / giver / recipient relationship, occasion, specific action,
use and emotional meaning; not applicable fields omitted].
Exact printed text: [source text]. Exact outside overlay: [approved row copy].
Keep outside copy separate from printed product content. Preserve brand space.
Hands, when needed: [person / anatomical left or right / action / contact].
Generate product, scene, overlay and brand together under this brief's method.
Do not show guide lines, placeholder labels or diagram colors in the result.
```

For an open-page role, replace the closed-cover instruction with the supplied interior view and source-supported ruling/binding details. Do not use a closed-cover silhouette to constrain an open spread. Submit one candidate per authorized row; a multi-row request must identify each separate output. Keep submission count, requested outputs and actual returned files separate when the service does not expose internal generation calls.

## Measure the returned book/journal face

Decode and preserve the original file. Record actual native dimensions, hash, role and request/candidate binding before deriving QA crops or previews.

For a clearly near-front cover, locate the intersections of its straight face edges beyond rounded corners. Use average top/bottom edge length divided by average left/right edge length as a 2D apparent ratio. Compare with sourced W/H, using the task's predeclared tolerance and a justified corner-uncertainty allowance. Do not turn the example pilot's 3% tolerance into a universal product standard.

An oblique floor mat uses projective-plane and source-view review, not this universal apparent-ratio test. Foreshortening is expected; apparent W/H alone cannot recover physical W/H or justify flattening a correct perspective.

**A continuous cover highlight belongs to the cover. Exclude a spine only when a distinct spine plane is actually identifiable. If plausible boundary choices change the verdict, increase uncertainty or mark HOLD; never select edges because they produce the target ratio.** Keep hands, ribbon, page block and shadows out of face dimensions. An obscured corner is not a measured corner.

Bounding boxes, parallel edges, right angles or a forced homography do not prove physical dimensions or eliminate foreshortening. Use SCREEN_PASS only for the stated supplied-anchor consistency check. Unknown view, unreliable corners or excessive perspective/skew remain HOLD. Preserve a REVISE result when the observed ratio is outside the unchanged task threshold.

## Keep the other QA and owner gates

Check cover words, sample name/date, typography, decoration, material/finish, original brand treatment, hand anatomy, support/shadows and distinct role separately. Inspect headline at150px and necessary support/brand at300px, alongside full-size source comparison. A geometry screen cannot pass these other checks. Keep native-size exceptions, source gaps, genuine-customer versus illustrative status, retries, video and publication gates attached to the exact brief.

Record an owner's acceptance of a particular logo appearance or other visual difference against that exact file and scope. Later candidates require their own per-file QA and any owner review actually required by the current brief or failed-file exception gate. Routine technical PASS within authority does not add a new owner question. Neither the whole SKU nor future products inherit an earlier exact-file exception. Do not describe approximate generated marks or artwork as original pixels.

Use separate findings in the per-file QA record:

| Check | Record and act on |
|---|---|
| Geometry | Source W/H, visible face corners/edge lengths, apparent ratio, relative error `100 * (apparent / source - 1)`, threshold and uncertainty treatment, then SCREEN_PASS / REVISE / HOLD. Do not silently add an uncertainty allowance twice. Open views use source-view review. |
| Artwork and copy | Compare printed words, sample name/date, fixed versus editable fields, decorative marks and original logo. Inspect font specimens against native font samples; plausible lettering is not font proof. |
| People and contact | Assign every visible hand to a person; verify anatomical side, fingers, grasp, occlusion and shadows. Viewer's left is not the person's left. Record only visible defects; do not infer a finger penetrates a book when it rests on another prop. |
| Role and readability | Check the intended relationship/action and buyer question, plus 150px headline and 300px necessary support/brand. Check footer omissions and whether important ordering limits become too small. A clear scene does not rescue unreadable information. |
| Delivery and acceptance | Bind native dimensions, SHA-256, source revision, technical findings and owner decision to the same file. Keep illustrative status, source gaps and exact exceptions visible. |

Recheck changed files; do not reuse QA by filename. A contact sheet supports coverage review but does not replace native-file inspection. Continue already-authorized independent rows without repeatedly asking for the same approval; stop dependent retries when the existing recovery limit or shared-failure rule is reached.
