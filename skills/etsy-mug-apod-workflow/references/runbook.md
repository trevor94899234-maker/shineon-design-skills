# End-to-end execution and resume

Use this with the generic coordinator's current workflow. The sequence below supplies mug-specific checkpoints; it does not require redoing a phase with valid evidence. Each phase produces a reviewable artifact and an explicit next permitted action.

## 0. Recover and define the endpoint

- Read applicable project rules and current user instructions. Identify product URL/ID, selected design, intended market, current phase, output count, generation method, minimum/native target, role/evaluator requirements, allowed costs and destination.
- Recover prior answers from the decision record, not from guesses about a title. Verify actual source files and latest application receipts. Distinguish requested, authorized, attempted, accepted, saved and verified.
- Choose the requested mode: source/pilot only; research/copy only; N thumbnails; N thumbnails plus listing text; full private APOD build; resume/application only; read-only audit. A narrowed request does not inherit video/pricing work from a broader generic default. A genuinely full build follows the Integrated Coach/coordinator contract, including its required native video unless the owner excludes it.
- “All listing data” requires a field inventory; it is not permission to invent missing business policies. Record fields that will change, remain unchanged, are not exposed, are owner-excluded, or are blocked. Do not call four copy groups “every platform field”.
- Create a tracked goal only if explicitly requested and available; don't manufacture a new one on each resume. Preserve the existing goal's scope and real status.

Output: one current record with scope, identities, evidence links, decisions, dependencies and next action. No questionnaire if all needed facts are already available.

## 1. Bind the actual product and sources

Before substantive full-build research, obtain/reuse the actual design-exported APOD mockup for this product. A generic blank supplier cup is reference geometry, not evidence of this design's placement. A self-contained pilot may proceed with the evidence it actually needs without waiting for every later role.

Record each variant's capacity label, variant ID, artwork/template branch, original named print file and matching mockup hashes. Retain exact fixed wording and editable field behavior separately. Inspect original images visually at native size. Inspect transparency against white/dark QA backgrounds without replacing the original. Record original logo as an independent source.

Check supplier identity and matching catalog/media portal yourself. Read current official product specifications, care, source-view support and destination-specific delivery information when the planned roles need them. Keep source URL, date, exact quote/asset ID and SKU applicability. Do not generalize jewelry or a different mug's specification. Supplier material can establish supplier facts, not the owner's shipping/refund choices.

Output: source register and supported-claim table; essential missing inputs hold only dependent phases/roles.

## 2. Research and position

Use the existing chosen source order. For 627726 that was Keyword Vault first; it is not a universal replacement of every future source choice. Reuse relevant evidence with dates before spending additional calls. Genuinely new unanswered research-order decisions follow the applicable research Skill once.

Ask Growth/Coach for one phase packet: actual item/recipient/occasion, competing buyer intents, candidate terms and exact measured evidence, selected keyword rationale, supported differentiation, claims to avoid and image-role implications. Record estimates as estimates; unknown, missing, zero and `<20` remain distinct. Do not transfer search volume from an observed phrase to a newly composed title or use estimated competitor sales as proof of this product's demand.

Execute research in the authorized tools/budget. Discovery of another provider does not authorize new spend. Avoid research that cannot change the selected product's copy or role plan. Do not invent competitor or supplier evidence to fill a table.

Output: concise positioning/keyword evidence packet; unsupported claims remain internal TBC or are omitted from buyer copy.

## 3. Produce listing copy and the full field matrix

Prepare natural US English title, all applicable tags, full description, personalization instructions and any supported FAQ/attributes. Current owner/shop convention is 13 Etsy tags of at most 20 characters; verify the applicable editor limits. Keep title readable rather than stuffing every term. Tie factual claims to the source register.

For a personalized mug description cover the actual physical item and quantity, verified capacity options, fixed art versus editable name, supported material/care, ordering instructions, included/excluded items and necessary illustrative-image disclosure. Add delivery/returns only when supported and applicable; don't imply proof service, gift box, gift-ready packaging, buyer deadline or guaranteed name fit without evidence.

Map every accessible field: title, full rich-text description, tags, personalization instruction AND configuration, variants/SKUs, quantity/inventory, prices/currency, category/attributes, production partner/design provenance where available, shipping profile/fees/processing, policy mapping, fulfillment controls, gallery/order/primary, video, alt text if supported, and existing warnings. Inspect the real editor; do not guess hidden fields or click Push merely to discover mapping.

Use field dispositions consistently: `PREPARED_NOT_APPLIED`, `APPLIED_VERIFIED`, `UNCHANGED_VERIFIED`, `NOT_EXPOSED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `EXCLUDED_BY_OWNER`, `BLOCKED`. Each has evidence or a reason. A required blocked field keeps full applicable completion partial unless the owner actually narrows scope; never reclassify it as excluded yourself.

Routine copy decisions within the user's request do not require sentence-by-sentence permission. If an exact-copy human gate exists, submit one complete packet with differences and unresolved material items. Once approved, retain its exact revision/bytes and use it without asking again. Do independent image/source work while waiting.

## 4. Lock N roles and one useful pilot

Make one authoritative N-row plan. The [mug profile](mug-profile.md) gives a 20-role example; N and the actual supported roles come from the user scope. Each row has a distinct buyer question, exact outside copy or none, evidence/view, source variant, format, status and current candidate. Different backgrounds alone do not supply distinct coverage.

For a new visual method/direction use a representative pilot, normally the actual single-mug source requested by the owner. Check source fidelity and natural integration before repeating it. If a valid accepted pilot and subsequent-run authorization already exist, don't ask to approve another pilot on resume. If an owner explicitly restricts execution to one test, do not scale it.

## 5. Generate through the required route

Under this owner's current Studio workflow use the documented ChatGPT web Create image path and whole-image generation. Discover the available browser/control tools and verify the real URL/session. A label supplied by the user is not proof of the hidden backend model version. Preserve requested method and record actual visible provenance.

For each role prepare original references plus exact role-specific text, product/capacity/offer constraints, original-logo treatment and permitted view. Reference alignment diagrams are guides, not new original artwork. Do not use an accepted redraw as the design source. Avoid overloading a prompt with contradictory counts or stale file positions.

Before Send verify the actual composer text, completed attachment names/thumbnails and order. Preserve multiline input using the tool's documented paste behavior; do not accidentally submit while typing. Submit once, record job/conversation locator, row/revision, prompt/reference hashes and cumulative attempts. Recover pending output rather than creating duplicates after timeouts.

Download the actual native returned file, decode dimensions/format, calculate SHA and inspect the real pixels. Request the approved target, but report decoded native size honestly. Under the current Studio owner rule 2000 square is preferred and 1245 square minimum; a later explicit brief overrides it. No stretching/upscaling to claim the native threshold. Transport normalization of a reference is separate from editing a final image and needs pixel/provenance checks.

Output: per-role native candidate receipt. Failed candidates remain preserved, separate from selected current files.

## 6. QA, resolve defects and establish use eligibility

Check every file against its original variant/art/logo: cup/handle/rim/base, illumination/contact/reflection, exact printed and outside text, name placement, motif, logo identity/spacing, material, offer truth, native size, readability at native and thumbnail scale, role distinctness and props. Full-set uniqueness requires visual coverage as well as unique hashes.

Use independent reviewers/judges only where the task's instructions or risk require them. Preserve each evaluator's actual model/reasoning, scope, input hashes, findings and verdict. One independent run may inspect a batch and return separate file-bound rows; do not replace per-file QA with one vague set PASS. A builder calling its own review “independent” is not sufficient. Later model upgrades don't retroactively relabel earlier runs.

Apply [decisions.md](decisions.md) to separate technical findings from permitted use. Persistent systematic redraw stops dependent production unless a bounded continuation/alternative is explicitly authorized. A correction ceiling doesn't authorize endless spending. Batch eligible cosmetic exceptions require current explicit policy, per-file inspection and exact-hash binding. New material defects hold affected files; continue unrelated eligible work.

## 7. Apply the authorized private APOD changes

Follow the coordinator's actual APOD operations reference. Verify product identity and current state immediately before mutation; compare against the baseline for concurrent changes. One operator controls that product.

- Apply missing approved text using the actual controls. Verify all selected tag chips, the entire description/warning and the personalization modal's own save. Save/reopen and compare intended values. Don't rewrite text that already matches just to demonstrate activity.
- Upload only the exact eligible file list; avoid directory globs including rejected candidates. Verify each observed upload result. A timeout needs readback before retry. Preserve existing/source assets; deletion isn't implied by a new gallery.
- Select the intended N assets, order them, set the intended primary and preserve/properly authorize switching behavior. Save, reopen and reconcile every role to the actual selected asset. A displayed count or file-exists check alone is insufficient.
- Where supported, retrieve actual stored originals using documented page controls/assets and compare SHA with native sources. If the platform transforms bytes, record both hashes, transformation and visual mapping; don't claim byte identity. Unsupported remote download does not authorize private endpoint guessing.

If a source/owner gate permits local preparation but not application, stop at the prepared packet for that slice. A product request that already explicitly includes private upload/update supplies authority within its known scope; don't ask again per click.

## 8. Video only when required by the current scope

Use the accepted/eligible matching thumbnail set first, then the coordinator/Coach's current native-video workflow. Preserve requested duration/theme/text/brand and inspect the actual saved clip through its timeline, transitions and crops; zoom can cut a handle, cup base or logo even when the still was acceptable. Compare saved/reopened source and actual playback, not a tutorial modal. Record native dimensions, duration and real source mapping. A slideshow must not be described as real customer footage.

If the request is explicitly N thumbnails plus text, or video is owner-excluded, mark it accordingly and don't add this phase. Thumbnail exceptions do not automatically waive new video defects or an explicit video acceptance gate.

## 9. Final reconciliation and delivery

After the **last** gallery/video save, reread the main text, tag set, relevant nested personalization settings and protected fields. Earlier text readback can become stale after a whole-form media save. Reconcile counts, exact roles/order/primary, hashes, technical findings, authority and field matrix. Respect editor tag order separately from tag-set equality.

Deliver a concise final status, source/role manifest, exact eligible native files ZIP, clearly labeled derived contact sheet, field dispositions, readback evidence and material residual limits. Complete only the authorized endpoint; keep required unfinished work partial. Explain exclusions without repeatedly asking to reopen them. Use [completion.md](completion.md).
