# Keyword combination matrix

Use this reference whenever generating, grouping, selecting, or reviewing Etsy keyword and listing-intent combinations. It turns the Shine coaching principle of starting with the buyer and occasion into a repeatable MyGiftStyle check. It is a coaching and local workflow framework, not an Etsy ranking formula or platform requirement.

## Seven intent layers

| Layer | Decision question | Typical evidence or language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer | Who is making the purchase? | church member, congregation, family, ministry team |
| Recipient / role | Who will receive or use it? | pastor, worship leader, music ministry leader |
| Occasion | Why buy now? | appreciation, ordination, retirement, Christmas |
| Product | What is the item? | journal, prayer journal, scripture journal |
| Use case | How will it be used? | prayer, Bible notes, sermon notes, song planning |
| Personalization | What truthful customization is offered? | name, date, year, Bible verse, custom message |
| Buyer language / emotion | What does the buyer want to express or achieve? | thank you, encouragement, appreciation, faith gift |

Within these seven layers, explicitly record buyer/payer, gift sender, recipient/user, actual writer and eventual reader, plus occasion and use. They can be the same person only when supported; this role map supplements the seven layers rather than replacing them. A grandparent buying a journal for a grandson does not imply the grandparent writes legacy letters or that the grandson has a particular age.

Buyer and recipient are different roles. For example, a church member may be the buyer and a worship leader may be the recipient. Preserve that distinction even when the final query mentions only the recipient.

## Useful combination patterns

Use only patterns that stay accurate for the product and current evidence:

- recipient + product;
- recipient + occasion + buyer language;
- use case + product;
- personalization + recipient + product;
- relationship + occasion;
- milestone + product;
- profession or ministry role + gift;
- faith or use case + product.

Examples for a supported worship-leader journal context include `worship leader journal`, `worship leader appreciation gift`, `personalized worship leader journal`, and `prayer journal for worship leader`. These are research hypotheses until current evidence validates them.

## Candidate generation and selection

1. Identify the current product and uncovered intent gap.
2. Generate natural, independently researchable phrases. A phrase normally covers two or three relevant layers; do not concatenate every layer into a long keyword.
3. Mark which matrix layers each candidate covers. If the active packet schema has no dedicated fields, keep the classification in the analysis or proposal metadata without inventing incompatible packet fields.
4. Reject candidates with a false product type, unsupported personalization, irrelevant occasion, unclear buyer intent, malformed wording, or duplicate primary intent.
5. Cluster the remaining candidates by primary intent. Treat spelling variants and reordered near-synonyms as one family unless evidence shows a meaningful buyer-intent difference.
6. Build the owner shortlist from complementary families. The local Dashboard may request five to eight candidates, but that count is a workflow convention rather than an Etsy requirement.
7. Use confirmed demand and competition evidence only after truthfulness, product fit, lineage, and intent coverage pass. Keep unresearched phrases labeled as hypotheses.

## Shortlist quality gate

A shortlist is ready for owner review only when:

- every phrase is truthful for the actual product;
- the buyer and recipient are not accidentally conflated in the reasoning;
- relevant occasion, use case, personalization, and emotional layers are represented where supported;
- the set contains distinct intent families rather than cosmetic variants;
- any missing layer is stated instead of filled with invented evidence;
- no candidate is promoted to approved, researched, or listing-ready without the corresponding owner gate and evidence state.

The matrix guides the whole listing meaning, not only tags. Once research supports a direction, check that product, category, attributes, title, tags, description, offer, and images all communicate the same buyer-recipient-occasion promise.
