# Adaptable profile: personalized Christian-family housewarming gift

Use only when this buyer mission fits the actual selected product. The historical example was a personalized home-blessing art print. It does not establish specifications, artwork rights, sample quality, price, shipping or permissions for another SKU.

## Positioning and copy

Primary mission: a friend or relative gives a personalized blessing for a Christian family's new home. Differentiate the emotional gift intention, family-name personalization and the actual physical item. Keep American English natural; do not imply a divine guarantee, a recorded customer reaction or a verse citation for wording that is not sourced Scripture.

Lock the real artwork and exact fixed wording. Identify the editable field from the source editor. Do not assume a family name, initials, date, fonts or alternate blessing can all be changed. Model names on mockups are examples, not buyer data. A visible logo must use the actual brand source and pass its applicable QA gate.

## Twenty-role planning example

This is the historical approved HB-r3 coverage adapted into a reusable planning aid. N still comes from the current brief. Roles 01/09/16/18/20 were owner-approved substitutions in that case; they are **not** blanket exceptions to another task's real-photo, rear-view or delivery-proof requirement. Choose/confirm material substitutions under the active brief.

| Role | Buyer question / purpose | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | What is the item? Clear hero | Actual physical photo when required; a clearly labeled mockup is a proposed alternative only with the applicable scope approval |
| 02 | What does a personalized result look like? | Same artwork and supported family-name field; no invented customization |
| 03 | What changes and what stays fixed? | Exact native field contract; restrained outside callouts |
| 04 | What does the full design look like? | Complete front, readable art, no obstructing props |
| 05 | Can I inspect the design details? | Source-faithful close view, no invented texture or embossing |
| 06 | What does the blessing say? | Exact fixed wording; no unsourced Scripture attribution |
| 07 | What is it made of? | Matching variant material/finish evidence |
| 08 | What size will arrive? | Verified physical width/height/units; source geometry, not a lifestyle mockup's apparent scale |
| 09 | How is it displayed? | Verified mounting facts; front plus text if allowed, never an invented rear view |
| 10 | Who is this for? | Christian-family context without fake customer demographics |
| 11 | What can a friend/relative give? | Illustrative gifting intention; realistic product scale/contact |
| 12 | Why this occasion? | New-home context; decorative boxes do not imply included packaging |
| 13 | How does it fit daily life? | Product visibly used in a plausible home setting, no testimonial |
| 14 | What quiet/reflective use might it suit? | Suggested setting; no invented included devotional content |
| 15 | What feeling does the gift express? | Gift intention rather than a promised recipient reaction |
| 16 | How is it produced? | Made-to-order only if verified; no invented lead time |
| 17 | How do I enter personalization? | Exact supported instructions and tested fit limitations |
| 18 | Physical item or download? | Verified delivery type; does not replace required shipping proof without scope approval |
| 19 | What item/quantity is included? | Actual single-item/bundle and supplied hardware facts |
| 20 | Which styling elements are excluded? | Distinguish supplied item/accessories from props |

Check overlap: roles 19/20 must answer different content-versus-prop questions; roles 10–15 need distinct compositions and information. Recoloring one room does not create six useful roles. If evidence supports fewer roles, show the gap and propose meaningful alternatives rather than count decorative duplicates.

## Historical case lessons, not defaults

Product 627699 completed the requested **private** 20-image/text scope on 2026-10-28. All 20 delivered native images were 1254 square; the owner's minimum was 1245 and preferred request 2000. Twenty exact-file appearance exceptions remained separate from technical fidelity findings. Twenty selected remote originals matched native hashes; ordered role 01 was primary. Title, description, 13 tags and required family-name instructions were saved/reopened. Shipping was explicitly owner-deferred; Etsy publication was not done.

The source was ChatGPT web image creation. The underlying image model/version was not exposed, so “Image 2.0 verified” would have been an unsupported claim. This applies to evidence reporting, not an alternative route permission.

The case required 34 retained candidates, not 20 calls. Extra attempts for particular roles were explicit one-off grants. These numbers, exceptions, 8×10 dimensions, aluminum/finish/mounting facts, $32.99 price and shipping decisions must never be imported into a new SKU as defaults.

Useful improvements: one current state rather than contradictory historical summaries; retrieve known answers once; one-role prompts with original references; batch real exception reviews; verify post-timeout state; count selected roles and actual hashes rather than filenames; complete private scope without repeatedly reopening an owner-excluded shipping task.
