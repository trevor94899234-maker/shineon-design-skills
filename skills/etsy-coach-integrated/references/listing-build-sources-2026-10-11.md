## Measurement for the zero-ad test

- Etsy shop conversion rate = orders / visits ×100. Orders are completed purchases, not item count; visits are browsing sessions. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628207-Shop-Stats-Glossary and https://www.etsy.com/hk-en/seller-handbook/article/219715875943
- Listing-level metrics documented in Stats: views, favorites, orders, revenue. Orders divided by listing views must be labelled orders-per-listing-view, not Etsy shop conversion. Visits refresh multiple times daily and may fall after bot filtering; listing views update in real time. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015774268-How-to-Use-Etsy-Stats-for-Your-Shop
- The Etsy search traffic category excludes Etsy Ads and Etsy-app search; app activity is grouped under Etsy app and other Etsy pages. It is not all organic Etsy search traffic. Same Stats glossary.
- Search Visibility is a quality-insight/recommendation page; the cited help does not document it as an organic impressions/CTR dashboard. Do not invent organic CTR using views or visits. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/25869947521175-How-to-Use-the-Etsy-Search-Visibility-Page
- Paid Etsy Ads has its own ad views/clicks/click rate. Keep separate from organic tests and eRank query CTR. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034223613-How-to-Review-the-Performance-of-Your-Etsy-Ads
