# Native print and editable-master gate

For a physical-product series extended to digital products, bind each SKU/variant independently. Preserve original source exports and hashes; distinguish named flattened composites, nameless artwork and independently editable text masters. A native personalization field in one APOD product does not make a derived PNG editable or establish another SKU's fields.

Measure actual raster pixels and target physical size; compute effective PPI from placed image dimensions. A correctly sized PDF does not add raster detail. Record requested versus returned aspect ratio, margins, crop and any resampling. Never stretch a returned ratio to fit a tag or call upscaled pixels native. If centered with extra margins, disclose them and retain design-review status until approved.

Separate original blessing copy from quoted scripture; do not invent a Bible attribution. Keep customer print files, previews and owner/source material separate. Before a final personalized download, verify editable name layers or the actual customization workflow, short/long/accented names, adequate native print quality, source-supported bleed/safe areas, 100% print/cut/punch/writing tests where applicable, instructions, rights and a reopened final package.

Case evidence: 19 October 2026 a nominal 8×10 art print had 1122×1402 native pixels (~140 PPI), a 5×7 card 1060×1484 (212 PPI), and a 2×3.5 tag received the wrong raster ratio and was placed undistorted with extra margins. All three were real new review designs, not final sellable print masters. Good rendered PDF appearance alone did not close print/editability tests.
