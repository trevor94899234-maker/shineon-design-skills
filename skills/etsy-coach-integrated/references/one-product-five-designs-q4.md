# One product, five original Q4 designs

Workflow revision: 2026.10.09.3. Apply under the current Integrated Etsy Coach. Reuse its evidence, consultation, product-truth and owner-control gates; this reference supplies an execution sequence, not a new coach, ranking system or permission.

## 1. Recover evidence before researching again

- Inventory the existing raw research, full query ledger, clean pool, dated corrections, rejected rows, product records and prior decisions. Preserve originals and stable source IDs. Record unread or inaccessible inputs; a summary is not a substitute for the raw record.
- Join each selected query only to its exact normalized phrase and compatible provider, feature, market, platform and time window. Preserve the displayed metric name and qualifier. Average monthly searches, a selected calendar month's searches, recent-period estimates and shop/listing totals are different measures.
- Keep Unknown, unavailable, null/missing, <20, a numerical zero and unmeasured wording distinct. Related phrases cannot donate numbers. Never fill a current missing value from an older observation without labeling it as a separate historical observation.
- Do not ban all low-volume or Unknown phrases. Preserve a truthful identity term or bounded exploratory test when buyer fit and intent justify it; record its limited role, opportunity cost and the first-party signal that would change the decision. A <20 estimate is neither zero demand nor evidence of a winning niche.
- Evaluate freshness relative to the Q4 decision: capture observation date, data period, geography, seasonality and material source changes. Do not call a historical peak current demand or a global result US demand. Explain what a new query would change; fill only decision-critical gaps within the user's authorized research scope.
- Reuse the supporting research and pool-cleaning skills by name when needed. Preserve their evidence classes and schemas; do not copy their workflows into this reference.

## 3. Select exactly one product carrier

Make the recommendation explicitly and compare the closest alternatives using current evidence. Retain accepted/rejected/held options and reasons. Select one carrier/SKU and a coherent buyer task; five designs are alternatives on that product, not five unrequested products.

Check separately:
- Buyer and sender; recipient, writer and eventual reader; gift/use occasion and actual Q4 timing.
- Whether that person can realistically use the product. A 200-lined-page journal does not become a guided prayer book, devotional, completed family legacy book or child activity workbook because of cover text.
- Supplier and exact SKU/variant, material, print process, printable area/template, physical size, page/interior specifications, personalization fields, accessories and packaging.
- Current cost/fulfillment evidence: base item, decoration, packaging, supplier shipping to the intended market, production and delivery windows, and applicable fees/taxes/discounts/returns assumptions.
- Reuse of existing inventory and risk of cannibalizing the same buyer intent; owner work and launch lead time.

Use claim-specific authority: current supplier records for manufacture, current owner records for the offer, exact-source research for demand, and official Etsy sources for policy. Never borrow supplier facts from a competitor's appearance or name a supplier based on a similar mockup.

If template/specifications are missing, select only provisionally for concept work and keep production-ready status blocked. If material costs, selling price or relevant fees are unknown, label contribution margin Unknown. A typical historical USD 49.95 price, competitor revenue or attractive demand signal cannot establish this SKU's profitability. Show assumptions for any scenario; do not promise profit.

## 4. Design five genuinely distinct alternatives

Create five numbered original concepts for the selected product. For each, record buyer/recipient/occasion fit, design proposition, visual language, layout/hierarchy, imagery, palette, exact fixed text, permitted personalization and source/rights notes.

Use separate identifiers for current deliverables and source-brief lineage, such as Q4-01 versus D30. Keep an explicit many-to-many lineage map; never reuse a new design ordinal as an old brief ID. Bind each final file/version/hash to its actual copy and motif rather than carrying an earlier brief description forward after the rendering changes.

Distinguish concepts in multiple substantive design dimensions, such as composition, typographic structure, central motif, illustration approach or narrative. Recoloring, changing a name, moving the same motif or swapping a seasonal prop does not create a new concept. Compare the full set for near-duplicates before rendering.

Draw on general buyer needs and broad visual conventions, not competitors' distinctive compositions, illustrations, slogans or finished artwork. Treat competitor examples as research evidence, never a tracing template. Check text/quotation permissions and exact wording when relevant; do not infer a license from availability online. Do not claim originality is legally certified.

For known name-only journals, use actual permitted name personalization. Do not add selectable verses, dates, messages, finishes or interior content without a current SKU record. Mark sample names as examples, not actual customers.

## 5. Produce actual images and classify each artifact

A design brief, prompt, contact-sheet placeholder or file path is not a finished visual deliverable. Render five separate images and inspect the actual exported files. Retain each prompt, input assets, permitted editing method, native generated file and versioned export.

Keep this original-artwork task separate from any concurrent listing-image batch or seasonal/new-buyer-angle variant. Track each task's approved brief, artwork/source IDs, file versions and open gates; a shared product does not merge their completion or acceptance states.

Record the current authorized image-generation route. If an exact model version is required, verify that the available tool exposes it rather than inferring it from generic metadata or a requested prompt. Honor a later relaxation of that task-specific requirement without claiming the unspecified tool became the formerly requested model.

Label artifacts accurately:
- Concept artwork: demonstrates the proposed design; may still lack a supplier template.
- Print artwork: flat decoration asset on the verified supplier template, with measured dimensions, print area, bleed/safe area, color/export requirements and resolution checked. A perspective mockup is not print artwork.
- Editable master: proven editable text/layers in the actual file or app, including appropriate short/long-name tests. A flattened PNG is not editable.
- Marketing mockup: illustrates the finished product faithfully; props, apparent finishes and accessories must not imply unconfirmed inclusions.
- Real product photograph: a photo of a physical finished item; an AI-generated photorealistic scene does not qualify.

Use Etsy POD Image Prompts for product-locked marketing imagery and Canva Personalizable Product Art only when an editable Canva/print-art task is actually needed and authorized. Do not turn a request for five design visuals into five full listing-image batches. Product accuracy outranks visual drama.

For reusable personalized production work, plan independently editable name text separately from fixed art and fixed wording from the start. Deliver and test the actual editable master when that is in scope. If only a raster design master exists, report that limitation and keep reusable-personalization production readiness open; do not claim editable layers, supplier approval or spot-color separation from RGBA metadata.

Record native generated image, edited/nameless fixed artwork, editable name master and rendered composite separately for each asset, with source lineage and hashes. Prove a single complete name input in the actual editable object, record reproducible font/rendering dependencies, and render short, representative and long names. A successful two-asset demonstration does not certify the other three or establish a supplier character limit. Preserve earlier PASS records with their original hashes; reopen affected visual, copy/brief and editability checks for every changed artifact.

Never label an enlarged source as native higher-resolution detail. Verify output dimensions, transparency when required, color profile and measured hashes; inspect actual glyphs, spelling, crop and physical coherence. For listing thumbnails, inspect full-size, 300 px and 150 px. Require only role-appropriate text readability, not every tiny cover detail at 150 px.

Keep requested prompt targets, measured pixel geometry and supplier physical specifications separate. Preserve dimension pairs as supplied and label inferred units/order; product-artwork canvas and listing-thumbnail dimensions are different contracts. Resolve production-critical ambiguity against the current supplier template. For a claimed margin, record canvas size, all four measured edges, bounding-box method and alpha threshold; very faint nonzero-alpha residue is not automatically the visible artwork boundary. A prompt requesting 10% margins or monochrome art proves neither. Do not turn a chosen pixel margin into a universal supplier safe area, or RGBA/color samples into verified single-ink print separation.

Before a current Etsy publication-readiness judgment, recheck official requirements. Additional computer-generated images may demonstrate customization. The generic production-partner mockup allowance does not remove the personalized-item rule.

## 7. Obtain independent evidence and visual judgments

Give an independent reviewer the raw evidence packet, current product lock, exact five artifact files, permitted personalization and user brief. Withhold the creator's preferred winner and expected verdict. A second persona in the same response is not an independent run.

Request distinct verdicts:
1. Evidence/product gate: exact-query fit and period, freshness/Unknown treatment, buyer–recipient–writer–occasion coherence, supplier/spec/cost limits, feasible capacity and supported selection.
2. Visual gate: real differences across all five, original design direction, exact text/personalization, hierarchy/readability, product fidelity, credible physical rendering and manufacturability to the checked extent.

Use PASS, REVISE, BLOCKED or NOT CHECKED for each gate and artifact, with cited evidence or visible defects. Numerical preference scores, if useful, cannot override hard failures. Repair actual defects and recheck changed artifacts and downstream claims. Record reviewer identity/run and what was actually inspected; no independent review means that gate remains open.

## 8. Deliver, observe acceptance and record the learning

Deliver one recommendation, five actual attached/accessible images, a concise comparison and the independent verdicts. State the strongest candidate as a test recommendation, not a proven market winner. Keep design quality, market evidence, production proof and owner taste distinct.

Separate concept/product-proof priority from permission or readiness for a commercial test. Before proposing a live cover comparison, verify the actual listing ID, matching buyer intent and traffic, protected experiments, inventory overlap, costs and remaining Q4 fulfillment window. For the same-intent comparison, keep other material offer fields stable and record changes and confounds. For a genuinely new buyer intent, prepare a coherent product/title/tags/description/images package instead of holding incompatible old copy fixed and calling it a cover-only test. A proposed review window or holiday cutoff is not a schedule, adequate statistical power or evidence of demand; insufficient compatible traffic remains inconclusive.

Track exact-version states separately: proposed → rendered → evidence-qualified / image-QA-passed → owner-approved → production-verified → published → measured. Some states may stay open. Record owner acceptance only from an observed explicit response or a clearly scoped approval on the exact artifact/version. Silence, generation instructions and the reviewer's favorite do not establish acceptance.

Recommend only one next step that fits the remaining weekly capacity, with a measurable first-party signal and a sensible review/stop condition. Insufficient exposure is inconclusive, not proof of no demand. Do not publish, order samples, spend money, connect accounts, transmit private information or start recurring monitoring without the applicable authorization.

Preserve Etsy metric names and scope: shop Visits and official shop conversion are distinct from per-listing Views and Orders. Prefer raw Orders and Views for the same listing, period and timezone. If calculating Orders/Views, label it a custom diagnostic, not Etsy conversion or unique-buyer conversion; never divide one listing's orders by shop Visits. Retain numerator, denominator, listing ID, interval, timezone and refresh/capture time; do not calculate on zero or mismatched denominators. Verify the current fields in [Etsy Stats Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015774268-How-to-Use-Etsy-Stats-for-Your-Shop) and [Stats Glossary](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628207-Shop-Stats-Glossary); checked 9 October 2026.
