## 1. Audit before restarting

When asked to optimize before execution, finish the failure diagnosis and save/read back the updated workflow before generating another thumbnail. Identify the failed layer: source/rights, original art, native text, render, scene fidelity, creative quality, export, upload or persistence. A supplier mockup failure is not automatically an artwork failure; a beautiful mockup is not production proof. Do not restart the entire pipeline or regenerate approved illustrations to solve a failed downstream renderer.

For original production-art editing, recent evidence supports preserving native source art rather than regenerating it. This does not authorize a thumbnail compositing route: the latest owner instruction requires complete built-in generation. Full-scene imagegen can redraw small leaves, waves and lettering despite an exact-product instruction. Removing raster lettering can smear white areas or alter surrounding decoration. Native text can restore exact wording/colour, but it does not retroactively restore altered art. Separate technical success, exact-art fidelity, creative recommendation and owner approval.

## 2. Bind the designer source before editing

Create one record per design/SKU/variant/print area: source filename/link and version; available original raster/vector/layered Canva master; native dimensions; exact fixed text; editable fields; artwork/font licence evidence; selected supplier/template/physical dimensions/process; approved change and unresolved facts. Inventory is allowed before the actual APOD mockup gate; substantive keywords, copy and thumbnails are not.

Prefer the supplied editable original or clean original art over a regenerated approximation. Open the actual authorised Canva duplicate and verify what is independently selectable. An editable link does not prove all text or motifs are native elements. Preserve original art position/proportions and original master; lock art after verifying fit. Do not extract contact-sheet panels, stretch one image across different SKUs or invent missing bleed. Font files and a designer purchase do not by themselves prove commercial/POD licence scope. Check the supplied licence and font family/style, required attribution and permitted embedding/upload/use; unresolved rights block the affected font/art use. Do not upload a font, substitute a font or install software without the relevant existing authorization. Visually available fonts must still match the chosen source or have a disclosed approved substitution.

## 3. Use the lowest-change authorised route for original product art

A. Native editable lettering exists: recolour only the relevant text, preserving exact words, font, layout and illustration. Verify complete selection and actual rendered glyphs, not merely toolbar colour. Keep customer name/date fields independently editable where the exact SKU authorizes them.

B. Clean name/text-free original exists: use that exact art and add the authorised native Canva/APOD text. Do not rebuild fixed text unnecessarily. Bind font, size, colour, line breaks, alignment and field mapping to the approved version.

C. Only a flattened raster exists: first seek the original layered/text-free asset. Imagegen removal/reconstruction is a limited-change fallback only within actual authorization, never a claim of pixel preservation. Compare full composition and vulnerable detail crops before accepting. If nearby artwork changes or white smears/ghosts appear, reject that branch, retain the original and report a specific source/limited-revision need. Do not keep regenerating the entire design or silently switch to deterministic pixel editing.

For the owner's identified LED pack, important message lettering must be white, including relevant fixed message and personalized fields, rather than retaining the designer's black text. Verify the actual native colour and exported/rendered glyph interiors on a contrasting background, then inspect a genuine same-variant supplier mockup. Do not whiten illustrations or apply this scoped requirement indiscriminately to unrelated products. White artwork, alpha and supplier LED illumination are separate facts. Physical ink transmission, brightness and glow remain unverified until supported by actual product/process evidence; avoid selling a rendered glow as measured performance.

## 4. Artwork/export and same-version mockup gate before marketing

Use the verified supplier canvas and preserve the complete composition. Distinguish source-native pixels, template pixels, export pixels, physical print size and effective source PPI. An approximately 1323 × 1189 source placed on 3540 × 3186 does not become higher-detail art; an observed 42%/medium warning must remain in the record. Do not erase a resolution warning by changing metadata or paid upscaling without approval.

Reuse Canva's actual short-name, long-name and restored-final-name checks, and current date/optional-field branches, rather than inventing another test loop. Inspect every fixed line and small date at actual print scale and the rendered product, including spacing to scripture references. Keep one complete transparent PNG per confirmed SKU/print area unless the supplier explicitly requires another process/background; inspect light/dark backgrounds for opaque patches, halos and remnants. Preserve separate editable master, production export and mockup status.

Create/export the actual same-product APOD mockup and inspect the downloaded result. Record product/artwork/variant IDs, template and source bindings. The mockup must bind to the latest approved export after white-text, font or layout revisions; an old black-text preview of the same product does not pass. For every reused supplier image verify reuse rights, exact variant/version, render-versus-real-photo provenance and whether depicted accessories are included. Only after this gate passes, follow Keyword Vault → necessary research → title/full description/13 tags → image production. Failed mockup generation stays a renderer/platform blocker unless art-specific evidence establishes otherwise. Do not claim production-ready from a supplier preview alone.

Do not repair even one thumbnail letter or logo in Canva, add overlays afterward, or use a separate background/cutout/source-pixel composite. If the same fidelity, wording, branding or physical-integration defect repeats, stop expansion and HOLD that thumbnail; do not silently fall back to Canva, native overlays, cutouts, source-pixel compositing or a supplier-only card. Preserve valid supplier evidence separately without counting it as the required generated scene or missing physical photograph.
