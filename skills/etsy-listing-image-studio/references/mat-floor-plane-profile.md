## 1. Bind product, art and atmosphere separately

Inspect readable originals before generation. Record product/variant/artwork revision, source filename/hash, purpose and observed details:

| Source | Authority it supplies | It does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Same-product supplier/APOD front mockup | Complete physical outline, visible edge and source-rendered layout | Physical sample photography, exact real-world scale or unseen backing |
| Original artwork/template and named preview | Fixed words, editable sample field, type arrangement, botanical motifs/star/dots/rule and colors | Floor perspective or material tests; an unnamed static asset must not erase an authoritative sample name |
| Same-product lifestyle reference | Visible product appearance and supported use context | Permission to copy its room/camera into every role or invent new product features |
| Mood/room reference or earlier AI candidate | Ambient composition/lighting only, when allowed | Product truth, revised artwork, logo or a new accepted source master |
| Original brand asset | Exact full mark/wordmark/tagline source | Proof a generator reproduced its original pixels |
| Catalog/owner evidence | Dimensions, material, print/care/quantity/availability within stated scope | A physically measured image or untested outdoor, waterproof, washable or slip-prevention performance |

Write fixed and editable content separately. For example, a task may personalize only the family-name wording while blessing, botanical art and closing stay fixed. Do not silently add font, date, color or phrase options. Record whether gold is a printed color rather than foil, if supported. Do not invent coir, rubber, thick piping, raised lettering or a textile macro from a flat design reference.

## 3. Floor geometry and contact

For an oblique lifestyle view:

- Place the mat on the same physical floor plane as the threshold and nearby feet. Its projected rectangle, depth and perspective must be coherent with the camera and floor. Rotation on the floor is allowed; its edges need not be parallel to every grout line.
- Printed words, borders and botanical elements share the mat's **same projective plane**. They foreshorten with it. Reject a trapezoidal mat carrying independent front-facing text, upright art pasted onto the floor, or a perfect front-facing rectangle in an incompatible oblique scene.
- Keep a plausible thin edge and contact shadow tied to the floor and lighting. Check lifted/floating corners, impossible shadow gaps, curled thick edges not supported by source, and foot/hand scale.
- For a full-product role, identify all four corner turns and edge continuity. Distinguish actual hand/knee occlusion, a corner hidden by a prop, an uncertain boundary and a corner cropped by the image frame. If a required corner is invisible, mark that check HOLD; do not infer it from a rectangle. A task-specific accepted partial edge overlap does not become universal approval to hide corners.
- Assign every visible hand/foot to a person, action and contact point. Do not let hands pierce the mat, point to unrelated artwork when the role is name inspection, or cover defects to obtain a pass. Record the real buyer action rather than imposing a stronger unrequested gesture.
- Keep digital overlay and brand in a separate readable area. They are not printed on the mat, floor or customer's clothing.

Use `FLOOR_PLANE_REVIEW` for this bounded visual consistency assessment. It is not physical calibration. Apparent W/H of an oblique mat is **not** its physical W/H; no universal apparent-ratio percentage can pass/fail all floor angles. A forced homography can map almost any quadrilateral to a rectangle and is not proof of correct dimensions. Source-view correspondence, coherent corners/edges/print perspective, contact and uncertainty must be inspected together.

For a genuine true-overhead dimension card, a sourced physical rectangle can support a disclosed near-front ratio screen with task-appropriate tolerance/uncertainty. Do not stretch an oblique lifestyle candidate to hit that ratio or transfer the journal's 3% example. A front-facing information diagram is not automatically a faulty floor scene: classify the image's role first.

## 5. Narrow method exceptions need task authority

**Original-logo-only restoration:** allowed only when the current owner instruction covers it. Record the exact source PNG/hash, whole-asset proportional scale, approved empty digital/footer ROI, before/after file hashes and allowed use. Retain the generated native. Verify all pixels outside the approved ROI are unchanged and the entire mark/wordmark/tagline remains intact. Do not split, retype, repaint or re-proportion it, enlarge the ROI over product/art/text, or use this to paste/warp the mat. If downsampled/alpha-composited, call it a verified source-derived logo, not unmodified native pixels. Missing permission or a dirty/overlapping ROI blocks this modification only.

**Separate original-art inset:** 627772 row12 had explicit approval for an original-product botanical crop in a separate digital information area, with no repaint and downsampling only if needed. A future row needs its own current-scope method authority. Keep the whole main mat/scene web-generated; do not paste the crop onto its product plane. Label the inset as source artwork/detail as appropriate, not finished-product photography or material texture proof. Record crop source/hash/coordinates, transformations, inset ROI and comparison outside the permitted region. The old row12 approval is not a general compositing license.

These method grants authorize bounded operations, not acceptance of unseen files. Technical QA and any exact-file owner exception remain separate. Existing acceptance never carries to changed bytes, a new revision, another product or video by implication.

## 6. Reusable one-row request and QA receipt

```text
Create ONE complete square listing image for [row / buyer question].
Same-product physical reference: [file + supported details].
Original print source: [file; fixed exact words, sample field, motifs/layout].
Brand source: [file]. Atmosphere reference, if any: [file, atmosphere only].
Camera and action: [supported oblique/overhead view; person's actual action].
The mat lies on the scene's floor plane with coherent corners, foreshortening,
contact shadows and scale. All printed art/text follows that same plane.
Preserve the full product view required by this row; no floating front-facing
rectangle, upright pasted art, invented thickness or new material features.
Exact digital overlay: [locked copy or authorized none], separate from print.
Generate whole product + scene under [method]; reserve [authorized brand area].
Canvas/requested native size: [brief]. Do not show reference-guide marks.
```

No prompt promises exact pixels or native size. A reference guide helps composition only and consumes an attempt if submitted for generation. For each actual final file record:

| Gate | Evidence and result |
|---|---|
| Identity/native | Role/revision, source hashes, submission/candidate, actual width/height/format/hash; native versus derived provenance |
| Floor geometry | Camera/plane, four corner observations, edge/occlusion/frame-crop findings, print-plane agreement; PASS/REVISE/HOLD with uncertainty |
| Original design | Every fixed/sample text line, position, type appearance, botanical shapes/count where visible, star/dots/rule and color against original; bounded finding, no unproven pixel-perfect claim |
| Material/contact | Source-supported thin edge/appearance, ground contact, hands/feet, shadows and truthful use; omit unsupported claims |
| Copy/brand | Exact locked overlay; original logo method/fidelity; permitted derivative/inset boundary and full source unit |
| Role/readability | Distinct buyer question/action; full-size inspection plus 150px headline and 300px necessary support/brand; limits disclosed |
| Authority | Attempts/budget still valid; human gate NOT_REQUIRED with basis or exact required/accepted decision; every exception tied to final hash/purpose |

Stop shared failing methods before repeating them. Correct within remaining authorized attempts; queue unresolved exceptions and continue independent rows. A new filename or a different Skill never resets the counter. A passing floor perspective cannot compensate for wrong printed art/logo; owner acceptance cannot rewrite failed technical checks.
