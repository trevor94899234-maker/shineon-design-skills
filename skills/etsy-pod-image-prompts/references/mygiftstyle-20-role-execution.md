# MyGiftStyle 20-role execution plan

## How to use this plan

Use all 20 role IDs for the current journal brief. These preserve the recovered 9 October buyer-function plan; they do not follow the numbering of the Grandson/Ethan style-reference folder. Read the batch workflow for current SKU/field facts and visual QA for mandatory style, logo, typography and anatomy checks. Keep the natural UGC photographic direction in practical-information images where feasible; factual clarity and exact product fidelity take priority. A generated scene is illustrative, never customer proof.

Every row needs a source-bound final file and an independent role verdict. Example overlay strings below are optional draft directions: adapt to the selected recipient, product, branch and current facts, preserve approved exact wording, and respect no-overlay briefs. Do not treat them as fixed text or promises. The common acceptance gates apply to every role: exact source product, genuine negative space, readable headline at 150 px and essential details at 300 px, exact original logo, credible anatomy/support, measured export and truthful provenance. Each role also has the specific gates below.

## 01 — Actual personalized physical hero

- **Buyer question:** What is this product and who is it for?
- **Scene/action/composition:** A real finished customized sample, photographed near-front in a believable human-use/gifting setting. Keep full cover/name readable, with quiet headline/logo space; avoid extreme perspective.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Exact version’s real finished-item photo, rights/provenance, current recipient/occasion and original logo.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “A Personal Gift for Mom”
- **Positive acceptance:** Product and buyer intent are apparent at 150 px; photo provenance and exact design pass.
- **Reject conditions:** Generated or supplier-only hero pretending to be a real photo; missing personalization; cut-off product; reference artwork substituted.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD slot 01 without actual finished-item photography. An illustrative hero-style pilot may be delivered as ADDITIONAL_ONLY, never counted as fulfilling this role.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Unlike 10/11/12, this is first-photo product recognition and source eligibility, not merely recipient, relationship or occasion mood.

## 02 — Personalization result

- **Buyer question:** What will the finished named cover look like?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Show the exact named journal held simply or resting in the reference-style natural setting. Give the name enough scale; keep support copy outside the product.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Actual named supplier proof or physical sample for the exact artwork/font/field branch; sample name recorded.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “Made Personal with a Name”
- **Positive acceptance:** Compare every word, name glyph, placement and font to source; the result visibly demonstrates permitted personalization.
- **Reject conditions:** Blank unnamed artwork used as a named result; generated wrong name/font; added corner on a name-only product.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD accurate result proof until the exact named source exists; do not pretend a generated guess proves the ordering output.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Show the outcome; leave scope explanations to 03 and input rules to 17.

## 03 — Editable versus fixed

- **Buyer question:** What can I change, and what stays the same?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Use the complete accurate cover in a natural tabletop scene with concise outside callouts pointing to genuinely editable areas. For a verified corner branch, show matched real PRINT/NO PRINT proofs without white card framing.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Current per-design field contract and real branch proofs where shown; fixed wording/artwork and name-font scope.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “Your Name. The Design Stays as Shown.”
- **Positive acceptance:** At 300 px the editable/fixed distinction is unambiguous; Love v6 distinguishes required name and explicit optional PRINT/NO PRINT corner using verified branch sources; Faithful distinguishes required fixed-font name and explicit optional PRINT/NO PRINT for 1–3 customer-chosen letters beneath the leaf, with its own verified sources and font mapping. Love stays 1–2 corner characters. Other confirmed corner branches use their own mapping.
- **Reject conditions:** Importing initials/date/font choices from another design; calling blank NO PRINT; showing incomplete corner removal.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** State only verified fields; HOLD unsupported branch/font examples. Do not infer A/B/C choices.
- **Keep this role distinct:** This answers scope, not the full ordering sequence of 16.

## 04 — Whole appearance

- **Buyer question:** What does the complete product look like?
- **Scene/action/composition:** One complete journal in a source-supported near-front view, naturally placed/held. Leave all supported edges and the single ribbon understandable; use minimal overlay.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Exact full supplier mockup/sample source and verified silhouette/accessories.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “The Complete Cover”
- **Positive acceptance:** Whole cover, name and body proportions match; no cropped edge or unexplained duplicate accessory.
- **Reject conditions:** Invented back/spine decoration, reconstructed unseen pages, perspective forcing cover redesign.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** Use a simpler supported angle. Missing reverse evidence means no reverse view, not a fabricated one.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Unlike 07/09, prioritize the complete appearance rather than macro details.

- **Required source inputs/facts:** Verified 200 lined pages and same-SKU interior visual source; current supplier source 17913 and specification 16999 are available. Page count alone is not a photograph.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “200 Lined Pages”

- **Reject conditions:** AI-created interior presented as evidence; generic notebook substituted; specification sentence counted as visual proof.

- **Keep this role distinct:** Distinct from 06’s free-writing suggestion and 13’s everyday scene.

## 06 — Free writing, not guided content

- **Buyer question:** What can I write in it?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Closed accurate journal on a natural desk with a simple human reach/hold or preparation-to-write gesture. Do not expose unsupported interior.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Confirmed ordinary lined-page journal and accurate cover; verified page count if stated.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “Space for Your Own Words”
- **Positive acceptance:** Copy clearly suggests the buyer’s own prayers/thoughts/memories without promising prewritten contents.
- **Reject conditions:** Calling it a guided devotional, workbook or preprinted prayer book; fake written pages offered as proof.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** Omit unknown contents; if ordinary-page specification itself is missing, hold the claim.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Answer freedom of content, not merely a change of location from 13/14.

## 07 — Material and flat printing

- **Buyer question:** What is the cover made of and how is the design applied?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Source-grounded cover detail within warm natural use context, with enough full-product context to orient the buyer. Use realistic light without false raised highlights.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Current SKU vegan-leather and flat-print evidence; exact source texture/detail.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “Printed Vegan Leather”
- **Positive acceptance:** Fine grain and flat ink match at comparable product display scale; material words are verified.
- **Reject conditions:** Coarse pebbled texture, engraved/debossed/foil/raised effect, invented waterproof/premium/durability claims.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD detail fidelity if source detail is inadequate; never enhance texture to make it look luxurious.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Unlike 04, explain material/process; unlike 08, do not use visual scale as size evidence.

## 08 — Dimensions

- **Buyer question:** Will the size suit me?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Near-front supported product in a quiet lifestyle plane. Place width/height arrows outside corresponding edges, aligned to the real object, leaving readable units.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Exact selected SKU physical width/height: scoped record 5.8 in × 8.5 in; later same-SKU specification 16999 also verifies 5/8 in thickness. Reconfirm changed products.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “5.8 in Wide • 8.5 in High”
- **Positive acceptance:** Arrow endpoints correspond to width/height; units legible at 300 px; no conflicting object proportions.
- **Reject conditions:** Using 1410 × 2220 print pixels as dimensions; unknown thickness; misleading hand/ruler scale.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD unknown measurements. Use only verified dimensions and omit unsupported thickness; do not estimate from a mockup.
- **Keep this role distinct:** This is quantified fit, not another general full-cover shot.

## 11 — Giver–recipient relationship

- **Buyer question:** How does this express my relationship with them?
- **Scene/action/composition:** A simple supported handoff of one journal between two adults, with each hand’s owner, wrist and thumb orientation planned. Keep hands on blank edges/back.

- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “A Thoughtful Gift, Chosen for Them”

- **Keep this role distinct:** Show the relationship/action, not repeat recipient portrait 10.

## 16 — Order steps

- **Buyer question:** What must I choose and enter to order?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Integrate a complete short buyer journey into reserved photographic negative space, using companion role 17 for legible examples if needed: select product/options → enter required full name → explicitly choose PRINT or NO PRINT → if PRINT enter the design-specific exact text (Love: 1–2 corner characters; Faithful: 1–3 letters beneath the leaf) and choose the available font for that field → check spelling/options → add to cart/order. Apply only steps the real buyer-facing Etsy flow supports; name-only products omit corner steps. Never copy APOD admin controls or invent checkout UI.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Current actual fields/branch logic and verified buyer-facing mapping; draft state identified.
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “How to Personalize”
- **Positive acceptance:** At 300 px the image set alone lets a buyer identify what to select, enter, verify and do next without a missing mandatory decision; exact branch labels/order match the verified customer-facing destination. Test both PRINT and NO PRINT walkthroughs. Name-only designs omit corner steps. No assumed live preview or conditional controls.
- **Reject conditions:** Showing nonexistent controls, universal four-step corner workflow, treating APOD preview as live Etsy checkout evidence.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD buyer-journey completion if Etsy mapping is unverified; APOD save/preview is insufficient. Draft platform-neutral instructions may proceed with that gate explicit, but cannot be called proven order-from-images completion.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Sequence/action, not field scope (03) or edge-case limits (17).

## 17 — Inputs, limits and spelling

- **Buyer question:** How should I enter the name or optional text correctly?
- **Scene/action/composition:** Show actual representative named proofs and concise rules in natural scene space; avoid tiny comparison grids. Include corner samples only on a verified corner design.
- **Required source inputs/facts:** Real native/supplier proofs; short/long names, complete glyph support, actual optional-text fonts and design-specific limits (Love: 1–2 corner characters; Faithful: 1–3 letters beneath the leaf).
- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “Please Check Every Detail”
- **Positive acceptance:** Name spelling/case/script preserved; stated limits and examples match actual behavior; optional branch clearly conditional.
- **Reject conditions:** Invented max name length, old A/B/C fonts treated as live options, initials auto-derived, silent glyph substitution.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD untested font/length/language promises; manual clarification for blank/whitespace or unsupported input. Current Love APOD tests accept two spaces and can render overlength programmatic input despite UI limit 2; font can default. Faithful caps ordinary typing at 3 but accepts programmatic overlength, spaces and digits; D/BB/WWW were inspected in all three fonts. Its font and NO PRINT defaults are not explicit customer choices. Explain required customer choices without promising automatic enforcement. Keep manual fulfillment checks.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Validation/examples rather than repeating 16’s order sequence.

## 18 — Production and delivery

- **Scene/action/composition:** Use calm product context and two visibly separate information groups for processing and estimated shipping. No invented warehouse or packed-order scene.

- **Example overlay, not fixed copy:** “Plan Your Gift Timing”
- **Positive acceptance:** Actual supported ranges and destination/holiday qualifications clear; processing not confused with transit.
- **Reject conditions:** Guaranteed arrival, stale general supplier teaching reused as shop promise, “check listing” counted as a complete answer.
- **Missing-fact behavior:** HOLD until current operational facts exist; never put placeholder values into final customer artwork.
- **Keep this role distinct:** Timing facts, not vague trust or policy filler.

## 19 — Included items versus props
