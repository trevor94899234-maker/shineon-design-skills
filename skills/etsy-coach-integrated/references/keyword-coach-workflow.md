# Integrated Coach across keyword research and allocation

This workflow implements the owner's explicit requirement that the Integrated Coach analyze keyword work throughout research, review/correction, selection and field allocation. It is not merely a final audit. Keep the same primary coach and product brief; load only the useful source lens.

## Mandatory source and outcome gate

Read and apply [evidence-release-gates.md](evidence-release-gates.md) before beginning and at handoff. Build the raw-source index, reconcile baseline plus overlays, and record every actual tag/researched title phrase in the phrase-evidence join before selecting or declaring missing evidence. Inspect actual competitor pages for product/intent fit; volume, concise copy and Coach agreement cannot substitute. Preserve uncertainty and use the separate stage labels; the independent outcome reviewer must challenge evidence gaps and counterexamples before any ready/final verdict.

## Before choosing queries

Resolve the buyer, recipient, occasion/use and truthful product fit using the existing seven-layer matrix. State the decision the next research could change and what evidence is already available. Use Dylan's customer-continuity lens (DYL-A002) or Jesse's distinct-mission lens (JES-V001) when relevant; do not turn either into demand evidence. Do not demand a complete cost or analytics packet to begin a truthful research draft.

Generate natural research queries for genuine gaps. Reuse valid existing observations. Query length is not the final Etsy tag character limit. No fixed lookup quota is required for a new advisory task; preserve any existing versioned Dashboard packet contract rather than silently changing its schema or quotas.

## During review and correction

For each decision-relevant observation, retain the exact query, source, actual country/scope, capture date, measurement window, metric definition and raw value. Keep observed zero, threshold values and missing data distinct. Distinguish a correctly captured estimate from first-party actual data; source verification does not make an estimate actual.

Compare like with like. A current US shipping price does not establish US keyword demand. Do not sum variants when the provider groups near-matches. For the dated distinction between top-level near-match aggregates and a table Match:Exact filter, plus provider time windows and denominators, read [official tool definitions](official-research-tools-2026-10-07.md). If a source lacks relevant scope or period, it may remain a language/product hypothesis but cannot support a current quantitative ranking.

Apply the experiment discipline in STA-A001 and the evidence qualifications on the selected Coach card. Correct erroneous claims or field choices now, rather than letting them pass until final review. If a source conflicts with current Etsy documentation or the actual product, retain the useful idea only within a clearly stated test hypothesis.

## 2026-10-09 provider and commercial-judgment delta

Read [current provider teaching and disagreements](official-tool-teaching-2026-10-09.md). eRank trailing-12-month averages, EverBee Keyword Research last-30-day estimates, unspecified EverBee Tag Analytics averages and Marmalead next-30-day forecasts are different observations. Grades, opportunity scores and tag occurrences cannot replace product/buyer fit or prove traffic. Use the [panel assurance](panel-assurance-2026-10-09.md) record for full review: fit + commercial intent + qualified evidence, with each weak/unknown/scope-limited signal explicitly disposed of. Search the latest available vault version and raw index before any new query; preserve prior snapshots, record a decision-changing gap and stop condition.

## Selecting terms

For every shortlisted or materially rejected candidate, give a concise reason based on:
- buyer mission and actual product/personalization fit;
- the evidence available, its scope/period and remaining uncertainty;
- useful coverage versus existing terms and own listings;
- the role the phrase would serve in this listing.

Keep a compact decision record: query or family, accept/reject/hold, reason, evidence ID, intended field and relevant Coach principle/source ID if it materially informed the judgment. It need not be a new Dashboard schema. A missing volume can mean hold or a small justified test; it is not automatically zero demand. A popular but inaccurate term must be rejected.

Do not use Coach votes, income stories, arbitrary opportunity thresholds, or a count of agreeing people to choose keywords.

## Allocate by the job of the field

- **Title:** make the item and essential objective traits readable. Preserve the primary buyer meaning naturally. Explain which selected language belongs here and why; do not force a ranking-first keyword position or every researched occasion.
- **Category and attributes:** select the most specific truthful entries and account for their existing matching coverage.
- **Tags:** use up to all 13 relevant available slots within the current limit, with distinct natural multi-word phrases. Check length and duplication. A shortened or recombined tag does not inherit the exact measured demand of its longer research query.
- **Description:** use relevant language naturally while answering the buyer's actual product, personalization, use, fulfillment and care questions. No keyword list or invented finish.
- **Images and video:** make the same selected mission and product facts visible. Media cannot add an unapproved product promise.

Use current official title/tag/description rules through [official Etsy references](official-etsy.md). Coach interpretation may explain the choice; it never overrides facts, measurement limitations or official policy.

## At handoff

Show the recommendation, the reasons for selection/allocation, corrections made, useful source IDs and remaining test hypotheses. A brief rationale is enough when the choice is simple. Do not simulate consultations with every Coach.

For a new listing, the coherent first draft can include the complete offer and media plan. For later changes to an existing listing, preserve the baseline and prefer one interpretable changed lever. Low traffic remains inconclusive; a full statistical study is not a prerequisite for drafting. Publication, spending and account actions still require their existing owner gates.

Read source IDs in [the Coach source ledger](coach-source-ledger-2026-10-05.json) and interpret them through [the bounded cards](coach-refresh-2026-10-05.md).


For the increment and added RJ lens, use [the current review](coach-refresh-2026-10-07.md) and [current ledger](coach-source-ledger-2026-10-07.json). Newly processed older material is not a newly uploaded video.
