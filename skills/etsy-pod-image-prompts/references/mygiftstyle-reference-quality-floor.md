## Fully specified prompt contract

Before generating or editing, fill each relevant item below with concrete values for this image. A vague instruction such as “UGC, photorealistic, premium, exact product” is insufficient. Do not include invented camera settings, model-version assertions or numerical similarity guarantees. Resolve contradictions before the tool call.

1. **Image role and buyer job:** give agreed role ID, one buyer question, chosen giver/recipient/occasion or use, intended emotional meaning and intended slot eligibility. Explain how visible action/composition answers the question. Identify whether the scene is illustrative or real photography; generated scenes are never actual customer evidence.
2. **Reference-quality anchor:** identify exact latest reference filename/hash and observed traits to meet or exceed (for example natural support, substantial product scale, readable side typography). State what must not transfer: its artwork, name, accessories, promises or unsupported interior. Choose reference traits suitable to this role instead of forcing every image into one template.
3. **Product/source lock:** identify exact approved design/SKU/version/source. Specify complete title/body/name, original font appearance, layout, proportions, source grain/color and confirmed material/process/accessories. List vulnerable details explicitly. Keep flat print flat. Use the actual named product reference; a verbal product summary is insufficient.
4. **Physical setting and support:** describe the real surface/lap/hand support, how weight is carried, where cover/page block/ribbon meet it, and the actual foreground/background depth. State the required product orientation and visible edges. A lifestyle context may be naturally hand-free when the role calls for it.
5. **Camera and geometry:** specify overhead/near-front/three-quarter view, compatible product plane, plausible perspective and crop. Define enough visible product to answer the role and preserve readable name/art; avoid unsupported angles. Match source-supported geometry rather than warping the product to fit an arbitrary scene.
6. **Lighting design:** name key-light direction, approximate broad/soft versus harder character, plausible window/lamp source, ambient fill and expected light falloff. Describe how that light affects the product, hand, table/linen, props and background together. Soft directional daylight or a believable visible/implied practical lamp can be valid; dappled sun, daylight, honey warmth and strong contrast are not compulsory for every season/design.
7. **Contact and cast shadows:** describe tight dark contact immediately under touching edges/fingers, plausible cast-shadow direction/softness and how it changes with height or fabric. Preserve source-supported material highlights/shading. If patterned light is used, its fall and interruption must follow plausible occlusion, height and surface orientation; it need not cross every edge, but must not stop arbitrarily at the product outline. Do not remove shadows, flatten all light or blur the seam simply to conceal a mismatch. If source and scene lighting cannot agree, redesign/regenerate the scene or use only an explicitly authorized light adaptation that preserves product truth.
8. **Human action and anatomy:** when people/hands are present, specify owners, visible hand count, anatomical left/right, arm-entry points, palm/back orientation, thumb placement, grip/contact and occlusion. Choose a meaningful simple action: held during reflection, edge grip, handoff or reaching for a pen. Do not force hands into every frame or have someone “write” on a closed cover. Non-contact hands may be legitimate when their action is clear.
9. **Composition and negative space:** plan product scale/location, action, prop hierarchy and genuine quiet regions for text and branding before generation. Preserve useful reference variations (side copy, top copy, detail focus, hand-free tabletop) instead of imposing a universal percentage/crop. Avoid either a tiny distant journal or a pasted oversized cutout that loses physical context.
10. **Typography integration:** give exact approved or clearly draft overlay strings, heading/support hierarchy, intended line breaks, source-grounded type appearance, effective weight/size and placement, all generated with the complete image. A requested font name is not proof of the generator’s actual font; verify rendered likeness/readability without claiming native editable typography. Use sufficiently robust, upright, brand-appropriate serif for the current reference language; a large regular serif is valid when contrast/strokes survive. Do not impose bold on every image. Supporting instructional sans text can be valid when it matches the reference hierarchy. Headlines read at 150 px; required instructions/units/options at 300 px. Reserve photograph space rather than patching a crowded scene with external-looking cards.
11. **Original branding:** identify the exact verified MG A5 input asset/hash, clear contrasting brand zone, proportional size and margins. Include that asset in Studio's current authorized generation references and require its faithful likeness within the same complete output, not a later Canva insertion. Its brand name must read at 300 px and identity remain recognizable at 150 px without covering the product/action. Do not authorize a redesigned, retyped, recolored or replacement mark. Actual generated geometry/color/text must pass comparison to the original; reference inclusion is not proof of pixel identity. If it cannot pass within the bounded retry, HOLD rather than shrinking, replacing or later compositing it.
12. **Truth boundaries and exclusions:** list unsupported interior/backs/packaging, non-included props, prohibited invented customer/testimonial claims, exact fields allowed and role-specific misleading shortcuts. A tiny disclosure cannot repair a false visual. Omit irrelevant negative-prompt boilerplate; target this image’s actual failure risks.
13. **Method and output:** state Studio's current authorized complete-output thumbnail generation route, actual requested format and artifact role; no Canva thumbnail assembly, split overlays/logo or cutout compositing. Do not assert “Image 2”/“Image 2.5” unless verified by actual tool metadata, or substitute a CLI/API/model/spend path. A reference or quality correction does not itself authorize deterministic compositing, third-party upload or publication. Keep opaque lifestyle exports separate from transparent production PNGs. Measure native output and final export independently; do not call upscaling native detail.
14. **Predeclared review:** specify the source comparison, selected quality reference, full-frame/150/300 checks and relevant hand/contact/texture crops. List concrete reject conditions before generation. Keep a prompt version and exact source bindings so the result can be reviewed against what was requested.

For a batch, prepare one shared immutable product/brand contract plus role-specific scene/light/action/type sections for every image. Shared wording must not turn twenty roles into twenty repeated backgrounds or headlines. Follow Studio's short-plan/confirmed-direction step and one initial authorized pilot; inspect its actual file before continuation. Check other role families, including practical information, within their remaining authorized rows before scaling that family. Do not require an extra second initial pilot, additional calls or repeated approval of unchanged rows. Preserve source and full-size/150/300px QA for every actual output.

## Whole-scene acceptance gates

First inspect the photographic regions of the actual complete generated output against the closest reference’s photographic scene, so text cannot distract from a flat or pasted-looking product. This is a review focus, not a separate layer or output requirement: do not remove text, generate an environment-only intermediate or assemble later overlays for that check. Then inspect the completed final alongside the selected reference at comparable display sizes, not just isolated source pixels. For each gate record observable evidence and PASS/HOLD/REJECT. Inspect the reference and candidate first at the same 300 px frame size, then at approximately matched product display height. Explain the visible light source/direction, lit-to-shaded material response, local contact and cast-shadow relationship; no numerical confidence or generic “same lighting” assertion replaces those observations. Do not invent a percentage-similarity score or “95% confidence.”

- **Single photographic world:** product, hands, props and setting share light direction, color of illumination, perspective, focus and plausible material response. Reject a flat frontal product pasted into a directionally lit scene, mismatched highlights, halo edge or unreal sharpness/sharp cutout against its surroundings.
- **Weight and grounding:** identify real support and inspect every contact zone. Shadows vary plausibly from contact to cast distance; no floating edge, detached shadow, translucent book, table-penetrating ribbon or implausible hand support. Shadowless diffuse light is not a workaround. Soft light can pass when it still explains shape and contact. A uniformly blurred offset outline is not a universal shadow model. A blurred grayscale lightfield is only diagnostic because reflectance and illumination are confounded; neither it nor equal RGB values proves consistent illumination across different materials.
- **Tactile product fidelity:** full artwork/name/font/material/grain match the authoritative product at matched on-screen product scale. Natural light can affect brightness without inventing coarse leather, cracking, engraving or relief. Hash-preserved source pixels alone do not prove successful integration; a scene-quality pass cannot excuse art drift.
- **Purpose and emotion:** visible action, intended buyer moment and exact cover message form one coherent story. Props support that moment rather than fill space. Do not claim the recipient’s real emotion, customer satisfaction or sales outcome. An occasion must follow the chosen listing brief, not the reference’s birthday/Christmas/graduation theme automatically.
- **Typography as part of the composition:** heading and support sit in intentionally quiet areas, have coherent alignment/line breaks and survive real thumbnail scale. Reject heavy text pasted across busy details, tiny/fragile serif, lost contrast or unreadable essential copy. Concise instruction layouts/callouts can pass when intentionally integrated and necessary; do not apply a blanket box ban that rejects legitimate reference-like information design. Generic white editorial cards replacing the lifestyle direction still fail.
- **Exact visible branding:** compare original mark geometry/color/text, placement and actual 150/300 rendering. Never let a weak logo in a reference lower the required brand legibility gate; improve integration without changing the asset.
- **Anatomy and perspective:** trace every visible hand to its owner and wrist, verify thumb/palm orientation and believable grip/occlusion. No forced hand count or requirement for hands to touch the product. Reject ambiguous/reversed anatomy rather than explain it away.
- **Functional truth:** verify the image answers its agreed role. A closed cover captioned “200 Lined Pages” can state a verified fact but cannot pass the actual-interior role. A holiday prop is not included packaging; a source-preserved supplier cover is not an actual personalized sample photograph. Quality approval never supplies missing facts.

If a scene fails, identify whether geometry, lighting, contact, type integration, product fidelity or narrative caused it. Correct the physical/compositional cause first. Do not merely strip the shadow, desaturate all materials, shrink the logo, substitute a different font or add a disclaimer. Version rejected files and preserve rejection reasons; no automatic batch expansion or delivery promotion.

## Reference examples and limits

All 15 unique images in the 24-attachment set were visually reviewed; exact identities are in the dated JSON source record. Use these audited lessons rather than copying a universal palette/layout:

- **03 name-only guide:** deliberate left-side information hierarchy, substantial right-side product and meaningful edge grip. Side copy and concise instructional sans support are valid; do not force all text into a top band or copy its name-only contract onto another branch.
- **08 daily thoughts:** directional illumination, visible cast/contact shadows and coherent table/hand/product integration establish a tangible object in an ordinary moment. Do not copy its exact color or make every frame hard/dappled sunlight.
- **09 vegan leather:** close lap/sofa context and material presence create intimacy; actual current product grain still controls. A reference’s leather texture is not the authoritative material texture of the new design.
- **04 prayer moment:** clasped hands express prayer without necessarily touching the journal. A closed journal held or placed during prayer is not a writing/interior demonstration.
- **06 graduation:** softer directional light is legitimate. Occasion cues belong only to the approved listing angle; they do not make graduation a default for every design.
- **10 lined pages and 14 fixed design:** large regular serif can work through scale/contrast; a supporting sans line can be coherent. Reference 10’s closed-cover caption does not satisfy role 05’s actual interior requirement. Reference 14 does not authorize editing fixed cover text.
- **12 elastic and 13 ribbon:** intimate sofa setting and a simple resting hand support practical-detail storytelling. Include only current-SKU-verified accessories and retain physically coherent ribbon/support.
- **15 travel:** a packing action and hand on a bag tell a genuine secondary-use story without requiring a hand on the product. Do not imply the bag or props are included.
- **07 Christmas, 11 faith artwork and 16 evening reflection:** hand-free arrangements are legitimate. Reference 16 uses motivated warm bedside lamplight; daylight is not mandatory. Keep light/material response consistent with the actual visible/implied source, and use seasonal cues only when relevant.
- **01 hero and 05 birthday:** maintain recognizable personal product and purposeful occasion/relationship framing; their source quality does not confer physical-photo provenance or transfer their occasion to another listing.

Some bottom-right marks on textured surfaces have limited contrast, and tiny disclosure/tagline text can be weak. Do not reproduce those flaws as part of matching the reference. Preserve the original asset while improving scene space and effective legibility. The minimum is comparable or better whole-frame quality under the current product/function constraints, never literal duplication of every visual trait.

## Regression and honest completion

The owner-rejected diffuse A/B source-preserved pilots remain rejected even if mechanical source-pixel or hash-reproducibility checks passed. A technical implementation can be reproducible and still look unnatural. A successful skill edit means the requirements are strengthened and saved, not that generation has become stable. The prior failed Love integration remains unsolved until a new actual pilot passes; filenames containing “source_preserved” are not proof of the method or pixel fidelity. Fresh outputs must demonstrate the gates; one good pilot does not establish all-role consistency.

Before saving a revised skill, confirm that it still permits legitimate variation (soft directional light, large regular serif, side text, a hand-free scene, concise instructions) while rejecting copied product facts, missing contact, lighting discontinuity, reversed anatomy, wrong original logo, unreadable text and function substitution. Preserve the full 20-role map and method/approval boundaries. Run a bounded independent read-only exercise on actual references and rejected assets; record its scope without calling it a generation test.

Report separately: cloud skill saved/readback verified; local-computer copy delivered/installed only if separately verified; Dashboard data changed only if actually authorized and checked; pilot generation/QA/owner acceptance only for actual files. Never claim stable output, a quality guarantee, local synchronization or account updates merely from writing requirements.
