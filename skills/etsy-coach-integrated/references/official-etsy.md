# Current Etsy reference layer

This reference records the official Etsy principles that the integrated coach should use as the policy and search-behavior layer. It was checked on 2026-08-29. Recheck these pages when a response depends on a current feature, rule, or search change.

## Search matching and ranking

[How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487) says Etsy takes a holistic view of a listing for query matching, including the title, tags, attributes, description, first photo, reviews, and more. It also describes relevance, shop quality, listing quality, customer service quality, language, engagement, and personalized/context-specific ranking factors.

Operational implication: keyword matching is necessary but not sufficient. A keyword recommendation should be checked against the actual product, the complete listing meaning, image clarity, customer experience, and conversion evidence.

## Keywords, titles, tags, categories, and attributes

[Keywords 101: Everything You Need to Know](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/keywords-101-everything-you-need-to-know/382774281517) states that titles, descriptions, tags, categories, and attributes contribute to matching. It recommends specific categories, all relevant attributes, all 13 tags, varied multi-word phrases, no deliberate misspellings, and long-tail phrases that accurately describe the item. Exact repeats of category or attribute phrases are not required as standalone tags.

Operational implication: the Dashboard should show category and attribute coverage beside tag suggestions, prevent duplicate or invalid tags, and prefer accurate phrase variety over stuffing.

## Title clarity

[New Guidance for Listing Titles, and a Tool to Help](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697) gives current guidance to state the item clearly, include important objective traits upfront, keep the title easy to scan, avoid unnecessary repetition and subjective filler, and include occasions or recipient types only when essential to what the item is. Etsy says the optional title tool is a starting point and that the seller remains in control.

Operational implication: do not encode a fixed “primary keyword must be first,” “first 40 characters rank more,” or “70-character rule” as an official fact. Lead with the clearest item and traits for the buyer, and preserve the main intent naturally when it fits.

## Search visibility and buyer experience

[How to Use the Etsy Search Visibility Page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/25869947521175-How-to-Use-the-Etsy-Search-Visibility-Page) describes personalized insights across listings, shop, and customer service. It highlights accurate categories, clear high-resolution primary photos, and customer-service standards. Its response-time note concerns when improvements appear in search, not a universal ranking or performance waiting rule.

Operational implication: a research workflow must not stop at keywords. It should surface missing listing facts, image quality, category, and customer-service risks when those are the actual bottleneck.

## First-party measurement

[Shop Stats: How Shoppers Find Your Listings](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/shop-stats-how-shoppers-find-your/22792705059) explains that Shop Stats and Listing Stats show traffic sources and keywords that brought shoppers to listings. [How to Use Etsy Stats for Your Shop](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/115015774268--How-to-Use-Etsy-Stats-for-Your-Shop) explains how to inspect Etsy Search traffic and search terms.

Operational implication: Etsy Stats is the first-party feedback loop after an owner-approved listing test. Do not use self-search position, eRank estimates, or EverBee estimates as a substitute for the shop's own traffic and conversion evidence.

## What is not official Etsy policy

Etsy's pages do not require eRank, EverBee, bulk-vs-individual research, a fixed number of candidate drafts, a fixed number of research rounds, or a fixed listing batch size. Those can be useful MyGiftStyle workflow conventions, but the skill must label them as local decisions and keep them adjustable.


## Targeted recheck on 2026-10-05

This addendum is a targeted current check, not a claim that every older linked page was re-audited.

[Newly Crafted](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/10603291042967-Newly-Crafted-Etsy-Updates-for-Your-Shop) documents native custom options, shared shop access, and two listing videos. It also describes tests of Marketplace Insights 30-day, 90-day and 12-month ranges, purchase-based price ranges and search conversion rate. Availability may vary: record the fields actually visible to this seller, rather than treating a baseline 30-day help example as the platform ceiling. The existence of a capability does not authorize configuring it. Historical claims that there are no staff permissions or that post-purchase external personalization is always necessary are stale.

[Etsy Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/) requires truthful product representation, respect for intellectual property and protection of buyer information. Buyer information from a transaction is not permission for marketing: mailing-list addition requires buyer consent and applicable policy compliance. Messages must not facilitate off-platform transactions. Do not use a coaching funnel example to bypass these constraints. Any proposed paid-item delivery flow must deliver what was promised without treating access as blanket marketing consent.

Coach promises of a ranking boost from arbitrary catalogue pruning, universal listing counts, exact-match-only discovery, or guaranteed income are not official Etsy rules. Follow the existing current-source gate and first-party measurement hierarchy.


## Targeted recheck on 2026 10 07

This check covers the claims materially raised by the newly reviewed videos; it is not a whole-policy audit.

- [Listing creation](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing) currently lists up to 20 photos and 2 videos. Capacity is not a requirement to fill every slot. Its 5–15-second guidance differs from the dedicated [video page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053206073-How-to-Add-Listing-Videos), which says 3–15 seconds, two videos, and audio removed. A 5–15-second file fits both; verify the actual editor before upload.
- [Keywords 101](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/382774281517) supports 13 distinct relevant phrasal tags, 20 characters each, and accurate category/attribute coverage. Long-tail refers to specific search intent, not simply a phrase exceeding 20 characters. Do not fill leftover space with nonsense or treat every repeated word as forbidden. [Title guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697) favors readable item-and-trait language; “consider fewer than 15 words” is guidance, not a mandatory field ceiling.
- [Truthful advertising](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1450964704111) rejects misleading product representation and manipulation of pricing history, discounts, listing features or reviews to trigger signals. Discounts must be genuine temporary reductions. No perpetual-sale or low-price decoy recipe is adopted from Jesse or RJ. Do not accuse a named shop of violations without the necessary price/history evidence.
- [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/) allow seller-prompted AI creations with disclosure in the listing description and require accurate production-partner/shipping representation. They do not require AI disclosure in the first line. A personalized first image must show a finished customized item, not blank-placeholder text. Original seller design is required; a supplier product or AI workflow is not permission to copy.

These official sources limit coach interpretations. They do not establish conversion improvement for MyGiftStyle.

## Personalization recheck on 2026-10-07

[How to Offer Personalized Listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344528-How-to-Offer-Personalized-Listings) currently documents up to five Custom options fields, with text, option-list and file-upload types. This is platform capacity, not a target to fill or proof that this shop supports each customization. Verify the actual editor and supplier SKU before application. Keep field limits, positions and finished examples truthful; existing owner authorization controls any edit.
