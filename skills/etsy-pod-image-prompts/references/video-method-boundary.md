# Image versus video method boundary

For a requested 20-thumbnail → 15-second video package, apply the existing per-SKU gate in [mygiftstyle-batch-workflow.md](mygiftstyle-batch-workflow.md#per-sku-twenty-thumbnail-gate-before-video) before starting video production. Require 20 individually accepted roles for that exact SKU, never a combined count across products; keep missing-role and fidelity/brand/text failures on HOLD.

Keep one-pass complete thumbnail generation separate from an explicitly authorized video-editing/compositing method. A successful source-preserving video repair does not authorize pasting print, text or logo after thumbnail generation. Use current thumbnail instructions unchanged unless the user specifically changes that method.

Transfer QA lessons, not incompatible production methods: same-SKU source locks; complete product and actual support; plausible hand scale/contact; unchanged source-colored edges and accessories; original branding; whitespace for readable labels; native versus enlarged quality; independent style/factual/fidelity/acceptance states. Keep labels, branding and arrowheads outside the product where the active layout requires this, not merely outside its central words.

Do not import facts from a neighboring journal, ornament or plaque. A source-supported near-front view can be sufficient for a limited pilot; missing interior evidence blocks the interior view, not every independent image. A referenced native-size request does not establish that a generation tool supports that resolution. Keep failed or superseded images out of completed sets.
