# One Etsy Coach, two delivery routes

Reviewed 2026-10-13; delivery-integrity extension 2026-10-16. Earlier platform/source checks retain their dates. Keep one entrypoint, source ledger and keyword workflow. Classify by what the buyer receives, not inventory ownership or digital design tools.

## Route before product work
- Physical/POD: a shipped object. Keep the existing APOD product → verified exported mockup → Vault/research → copy → role-specific gallery → APOD saves/videos/readback contract unchanged.
- Digital: a downloadable file or a verified access/template link. Use the digital gates below; do not impose physical shipping, a physical mockup export or exactly 20 images/native POD videos merely by habit.
- Mixed: document each component and fulfilment path. Verify platform support before selling a bundle; a recommended-product link is not a bundle checkout or automatic discount.
- A computer-designed poster shipped on paper is physical. A Bible PDF is digital. A Canva template link is not automatically an Anywhere Digital editable-layer product.

## Digital gates
1. Establish payer, adult facilitator, learner, occasion, learning/use task, actual rights and original content. Preserve biblical meaning; distinguish original retelling/blessing from a quoted translation. Age, duration and learning outcomes are pilot targets until tested.
2. Lock the real deliverable: file manifest, source/master version, hashes, PDF page count, sizes/formats, fonts, software/printing requirements, editable versus flattened content, instant versus made-to-order. Label each uploaded file as buyer delivery, preview-only or internal; keep internal notes out of downloads.
3. Open and inspect real files and a truthful buyer preview. Check answers, legibility, export fidelity, file size and download/access behaviour. Verify template permissions only within existing authorized sharing. A private file-only draft may be saved earlier if authorized, but is not sale-ready and does not bypass the substantive SEO gate.
4. After the real-file/preview gate, reuse the existing Keyword Vault and buyer-intent workflow. Preserve dates and metric qualifiers; obtain new queries only for material gaps. Do not treat an AI assistant's recommended keywords as measured demand.
5. Draft title, relevant tags, description, exact contents, personalization, download/use/print instructions, license and support. Distinguish household/classroom/church use; do not assume a classroom license. Show real pages and useful sample content; avoid invented testimonials or results.
6. Cost platform/payment fees, conditional Offsite Ads, tools/storage/material licensing, support, creation and version-maintenance labour. Separate sunk/fixed and incremental costs. No physical shipping charge for files; zero marginal printing does not mean zero cost. Keep markup and margin distinct.
7. In the actual supported digital interface, configure upload/delivery enablement, preview-only files, download page/PDF, personalization fields, recommendations and pricing only to the approved scope. The 2026-10-07 official Anywhere Digital guide describes file-only private drafts, optional blank price/category and skipping mockup; verify account UI. Do not assume native Etsy capacities apply to an external service.
8. Save and reopen: verify correct files, previews, delivery flags, field mapping, price state and links. Saving Digital is not updating Etsy; page recommendations may differ from delivery-PDF recommendations. Verify whether instant/customized fulfilment works rather than promising it.
9. Before delivery or multi-part repackaging, apply [digital delivery integrity](digital-delivery-integrity.md): verify the intended version, distinguish byte identity from content parity, and check archive paths across the complete set. Deliver a checklist and files/links with open issues. Keep private by default under the existing owner-operated Etsy publication boundary. No purchase, new legal terms, account expansion or auto-publishing from this reference.

## Privacy and rights
Do not auto-subscribe Etsy buyer emails. A useful free sample may have a separately authorized voluntary adult-email offer; never condition access to already-purchased files on subscribing. Do not default to collecting children's names/photos. Public-domain Bible translation claims, Canva asset/template rights, font rights, PLR/MRR licenses and Etsy seller-originality requirements are separate checks. Free resources are not automatically resale material.
