# Personalized concepts, briefs and delivered-design review

Use when a reference image should inform an original personalized gift, when preparing a designer brief, or when auditing the designer's actual delivery. Keep the existing Coach and product coordinator; this reference does not launch a council, another agent, research round or full listing build.

## Select the requested endpoint

| Mode | Useful completion | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Buyer/giver/recipient, occasion, intended feeling, new story/composition and meaningful proposed personalization | SKU, print shape and field support may remain TBC; no manufacturing, editability or demand claim |
| Designer brief | Exact fixed copy, variable roles, hierarchy/placement, reference purpose, original visual direction and known template constraints | A specification for work, not evidence that art or customer fields exist |
| Post-delivery audit | Compare the exact delivered revision with its actual brief/copy/layout and give actionable corrections | Earlier Coach work, source layers or art-content approval do not pre-pass a new delivery |
| Full product/listing build | Return the same record to the existing APOD/Digital route and its complete contract | Do not reduce or bypass its source, mockup, research, save or release gates |

An open new-series/product-selection request retains the main Skill's upstream Growth discovery route. A bounded requested concept does not need a forced SKU choice or substantive SEO work merely to express its design; disclose the missing production facts. Use the current project/owner art-generation and editable-text method, preserve existing source locks and supplied copy, and do not add Canva, API, paid-service or platform-write steps by inference.

## Turn the reference into a new story

Identify what the reference contributes: emotional moment, relationship, symbol, palette or visual structure. Separate that observation from the new design decision. Develop a different scene and composition that express the chosen recipient's moment; changing only color, names or a generic faith phrase is not meaningful differentiation. Do not trace the reference layout or imply that a wood-carved, metallic or three-dimensional appearance proves the new product uses that process.

State the buyer, giver, recipient, occasion and intended feeling separately. Let personalization do a job in that story: a family surname can identify the household in a journey scene; a sender line can sign a letter; a child name can identify whom a blessing addresses. These are examples, not mandatory fields or approved copy. Use only fields that suit the relationship and the actual product contract; existing name-only products do not acquire year/message options from these examples.

Plan the complete composition together: exact fixed title/body, illustration, native editable name and intentional space. Give the name a clear role and readable prominence without overpowering the message or leaving a large unexplained blank. Compare related batch directions by story, relationship, composition, name function and intended use; do not present cosmetic variants as different concepts. Treat emotional effectiveness as design judgment, not measured buyer response.

## Audit the actual delivery against the brief

Recover the authoritative brief, exact copy, source purpose and current delivery revision. Inspect the actual rendered file and any relevant supplied layer evidence; reconcile the delivered versus reviewed file identity before correcting another reviewer. Preserve the original delivery and review only the authorized scope.

Use a short comparison in the existing record, for example `brief requirement → observed delivery → buyer/design impact → exact correction → evidence still needed`:

- **Story and recipient:** Does the new work still express the intended relationship and moment, or did decoration change its meaning?
- **Copy:** Read each fixed line, salutation, punctuation and closing against the supplied copy. Identify omissions, additions or reordered meaning; propose any wording change explicitly instead of silently replacing approved text.
- **Composition:** Compare the actual motif, scale, order, palette and spacing with the brief. Name the affected element and a concrete correction, such as restoring the blessing below a smaller emblem or removing an unrequested dominant motif.
- **Personalization:** Distinguish recipient, sender, household, salutation and year. Identify which parts are fixed, proposed editable, actually seller-editable or evidenced customer-editable; sample text and PSD descriptors alone prove none of the latter.
- **Product and revision:** Check the actual SKU/template where known, full composition and print-area fit at the appropriate stage. Bind subsequent mockups to this revision; an art-only approval does not cover fixed text, fields or a future mockup.

Keep strengths as well as deviations. Prioritize changes that restore meaning, exact copy, name hierarchy or the product contract; do not redesign an accepted master merely to suit a new reviewer's taste. A useful correction tells the designer what to preserve, what to change and what proof will resolve the gap. If brief and current owner decision conflict, identify the specific decision needed and continue unaffected review.

## Field choices that change the brief

- **Optional content:** A church or year can be omitted only if the actual empty result contains no sample/placeholder or orphan separator and the remaining layout reads well. A balanced fixed layout may work; automatic reflow is not an invented prerequisite.
- **Finite choices:** Preserve an explicit Mom/Mum or Grandma/Nana/Grammy requirement. Distinguish a verified finite selector, free text and a proposed/unimplemented option; an untested dropdown does not block independent concept/layout work, and free text is not proof of a constrained choice.
- **Composite names and closings:** `Love, Oliver` in one source layer does not establish a baby-name-only field. Likewise, separate `The` / surname / `Family` source layers do not establish a usable full sender input. Specify whether the buyer enters the whole sender line or only the variable part, then verify the real native-field mapping.

For implementation, use the existing APOD [personalization and printable-fit matrix](../../etsy-apod-product-workflow/references/listing-field-matrix.md#personalization-live-preview-and-printable-fit), applicable project tests and save/reopen evidence; do not create another test ledger. Font, character limits, auto-fit, optional behavior and customer-versus-seller editability remain TBC until evidenced. APOD configuration and Etsy buyer-input mapping are separate.

Return the requested concept, brief or correction list with exact source/revision pointers and remaining dependencies. Keep simulated review, technical QA, owner acceptance and production/publication approval distinct.
