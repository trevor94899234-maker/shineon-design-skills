# Series ideas to verified production

Select one useful gift task and distinct role per extension, rather than treating repeated art on several formats as independent validated products. Bind buyer, recipient, sender, occasion and evidence to each SKU. Preserve original blessings as original text; do not label them scripture. A live existing physical product and a new digital design review remain different artifacts.

Route listing video production to Etsy Coach's source-locked video workflow, thumbnail generation and per-file QA directly to the local `etsy-listing-image-studio`, and editable print masters to the appropriate artwork workflow. Read Studio's current SKILL.md and applicable references; its standing route is ChatGPT web image creation through the in-app browser unless explicitly overridden by the owner. `etsy-pod-image-prompts` may supply supporting role/prompt evidence, but must not become a parallel generator or revive older built-in thumbnail instructions. If Studio or its route is unavailable, report the blocker rather than substituting. Reuse the technical gates instead of duplicating divergent rules here. Interpret current task scope at execution, not as standing permission stored in a skill.

For digital extensions, require actual native pixel/physical-size checks, aspect-ratio disclosure and independently editable personalization before calling a print package ready. A PDF's paper dimensions, nice preview or source product's native name field do not satisfy those gates. Record concepts, actual review files, approved designs, production-ready files, APOD saved products and published listings separately.

A useful daily improvement is a source-grounded regression rule from a real failed or accepted attempt, not another skill count. Keep only reusable methods and de-identified examples. Do not turn a transcript read into “learned every app feature”, generated asset count into sales evidence, or a pending upload into a completed product.
