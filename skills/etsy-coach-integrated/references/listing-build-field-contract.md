# Listing build field contract

Use with [evidence-release-gates.md](evidence-release-gates.md) and [keyword-coach-workflow.md](keyword-coach-workflow.md). This reference specifies executable outputs and acceptance tests; it does not replace their authority, evidence, independent review or approval rules. Local stage names and test conventions are not Etsy requirements.

For the owner-requested MyGiftStyle full-build trigger, [apod-ready-listing-build.md](apod-ready-listing-build.md) supplies the controlling strict sequence and completion boundary. Actual APOD mockup generation/export/inspection precedes substantive Keyword Vault research, copy and thumbnails; completed files alone are not completion. Actual APOD saves/uploads/video/readback are included only within the identified authorized private draft scope. Keep owner-only Etsy sync.

## 1. Bind input and version

Record run ID, date, shop, market/language, allowed destinations, immutable source pointers/hashes and exact product/design/variant/artwork IDs. Separate old artwork approval, current APOD branch, proposed listing copy, currently saved private fields and actual Etsy listing. Never inherit approval through a similar filename or a shared design name.

Use the complete current keyword vault, raw indexes, dated overlays and owner CSVs first. Explicitly state which are available and which original bytes are not recoverable. Inspect relevant exact rows and complete monthly series before declaring gaps. A prior narrow intake audit applies only to its recorded scope. Draft work may proceed while market-fit or production gates remain open.

## 2. One buyer mission per design

For every product fill buyer/payer, sender, recipient/user, actual writer, eventual reader, occasion, product, use, personalization and emotional job. Distinguish owner-confirmed positioning from a proposed test. Choose a primary buyer mission; keep alternatives inactive rather than mixing father, mentor, graduation, Christmas and every other occasion into one draft.

For a multi-design run produce a cross-listing matrix: primary mission, shared truthful identity terms, unique differentiators, existing own listings that may overlap, and the intended test. Shared product tags are allowed when accurate. Mere tag overlap is not evidence of harmful cannibalization, nor should all five listings be forced into artificially unique keywords. Investigate own inventory and first-party search terms before making that causal claim.

## 3. Source-aware selection and allocation

For every actual tag and decision-relevant researched title phrase record exact spelling, normalization, evidence IDs and raw metric qualifiers, market/window, product-fit, intent, evidence strength, field role, accept/test/hold/reject and reason. Composed copy never inherits exact-query numbers. Unknown, low-volume or threshold phrases can be truthful bounded tests; do not quietly fill all slots with weak phrases and call them proven.

Assign language by field job: category/attributes for actual structured facts; title for instantly understandable item and essential differentiator; tags for complementary matching; opening description for buyer relevance and item clarity; body for facts and friction; media for proof. Read actual category/attribute choices before deducting duplicate tag coverage. No tag change solely to manufacture variety.

## 4. Field packet: required rows

Each row contains current value and verified destination/source; proposed value; rationale; evidence status; QA; missing input owner; and next authorized action. Do not omit unavailable fields.

- Product and offer: physical item, exact SKU/variant, quantity and what is included, current art version, material, process, dimensions, pages/sheets, accessories, care only if verified.
- Category: most specific accurate category/path and actual taxonomy ID after reading live choices. A suggested path is not a selected category. Record handmade/designed-by classification and production partner separately.
- Attributes/materials: exact permitted values, confirmed colors/materials/size and relevant occasion/recipient where the current category actually supports them. Do not infer leather species, recycled content, archival/acid-free paper, waterproofing or page weight.
- Title: one recommended version and, only if decision-useful, one inactive alternative; character/word counts, intent and evidence rationale. Hard maximum differs from official editorial guidance. Preserve a coherent existing title unless the new version resolves a stated problem.
- Tags: 13 truthful distinct tags when supported, per-tag character counts and exact evidence join. A 20-character slot restriction does not require researching shortened variants before an honest draft; shortened wording remains its own unmeasured phrase unless matched.
- Description: complete customer-facing text, with product/buyer-focused opening, practical uses, item facts, personalization, fixed-versus-variable elements, contents, verified fulfillment/care facts and required design/production disclosure. Avoid keyword lists and unsupported promises. Missing operational sections stay in the work checklist, never in copy-paste customer copy as fake values.
- Personalization/options: required fields, fixed elements, examples, input limits established by actual renderer tests, validation rules and branch mapping. Platform field capacity is not evidence the connector supports or transfers that structure. Do not promise conditional visibility, live preview, selectable fonts or automatic validation until verified. A name-free input or missing branch decision blocks the order; no silent fallback.
- Price/inventory: current price and currency, owner-approved proposed price if any, component cost model, handling/fulfillment effort, quantity behavior and exact variant mapping. Supplier base cost alone is not margin. Bundles require complete second-SKU and combined shipping evidence; remain separate from this single-item offer.
- Processing/shipping: production plus shop review time, shipping origin, profile, destinations, fees, transit estimates and holiday exceptions. Separate processing from transit. Do not invent an arrival guarantee or import competitor promises.
- Returns/cancellations/customer care: actual shop policy and applicable displayed legal/platform constraints; proof-preview service only if offered. Do not add default no-return/no-cancellation promises to personalized goods. Policy uncertainty must be resolved before publication, not necessarily placed into an image FAQ.
- Media: the current image specialist's actual role map, precise source art, finished assets/hashes, dimensions, fidelity/readability and real-photo requirements. Requested 20 roles are not 20 accepted images. Track tried/rejected/QA-passed/owner-approved independently. Any video is separately scoped and verified against current official/editor limits.
- Accessibility and shop presentation: accurate image alt text if the current editor exposes it, readable images, shop section, production partner and AI-design disclosure as applicable; do not use alt text as hidden keyword stuffing.
- Final destination: clean Etsy title without an internal DRAFT prefix, per-field APOD-to-Etsy mapping, excluded control fields, owner review and owner-operated sync, actual post-sync readback if authorized.

## 5. Platform constraints registry

Read [the dated listing-build source record](listing-build-sources-2026-10-11.md) for this release’s checked official sources and stated scope. Reverify changed/current limits rather than treating the dated record as a permanent rule.

Before each consequential build, verify current official Etsy guidance or the live editor for fields that affect the output. Store URL, checked date, hard limit versus recommendation, contradictory official statements and account-dependent UI differences. Do not embed permanent historical limits or assume global Etsy UI is equivalent to APOD. Use the stricter compatible draft spec while a non-critical official discrepancy remains unresolved; do not invent unsupported functionality. Read-only official verification is separate from changing account fields.

## 6. Media handoff

Pass the same buyer mission and per-product contract to the current etsy-pod-image-prompts skill rather than copying its full procedure. For the current 20-role brief preserve role 01's finished personalized real-photo gap, role 18's verified delivery information need, and role 20's general useful FAQ scope. Do not force preview/returns/cancellation onto role 20 when the user accepted general factual FAQs. Still resolve actual shop policies in the overall listing checklist before release. Source-pixel compositing approval is a method permission, not a visual-quality pass, physical product photo or owner acceptance of every file.

## 7. Baseline and bounded experiment

For a new listing record baseline as not yet live, not zero performance. Capture actual first-party shop/listing metrics only when available, with period, denominator and source. Record price, shipping, photos, tags, category, title, stock and season/changes as confounders. Keyword-tool CTR is never purchase conversion. Do not calculate organic CTR without actual compatible search-impression and click denominators; listing views are not unique shop visits.

For existing listings preserve a before snapshot and change one principal interpretable lever when feasible. For new listings a full coherent initial offer is acceptable; do not pretend its components form isolated A/B tests. With the current zero-ad, 3–5-hour weekly constraint choose a small review cadence and task budget, not a guaranteed sample size. A local 7-day data-quality check and later owner-agreed comparison window are planning conventions, not ranking or indexing promises. Low traffic is inconclusive. Define what observation prompts keep/revise/hold and what evidence would change the buyer/keyword choice.

## 8. Missing-input matrix and status

Use distinct classes: owner decision; retrievable source fact; production/visual proof; unknown market data; connector/destination capability. Give exact missing item, why it matters, source/next step, who can supply it, and which draft/release stage it blocks. Group questions; do not ask the owner to provide facts that can be retrieved from existing authorized files. Do not block truthful text drafts merely because physical samples, final price or delivery policy remain unresolved.

Record stage separately for draft, evidence-qualified, text-QA-passed, image-QA-passed, owner-approved, APOD-private-applied, Etsy-applied and measured. Bind each status to exact version and scope. Run count/length/ID/description/tag/claims/branch checks and saved-artifact readback. Obtain the existing independent adversarial semantic review before any full ready verdict; do not expand a narrow source-intake PASS into buyer-fit or publication PASS.

## 9. Delivery and stop boundary

Deliver the full five field packets when five designs were requested, a short comparison and prioritized gap matrix, plus machine-readable per-product checklist and source manifest. Show concrete drafts, not only methods. Report exact applied destination and fields, or that this run only produced proposals. No silent Etsy sync, publication, price/inventory/shipping changes, order, payment or external communication. User-only sync remains a hard workflow boundary where specified.
