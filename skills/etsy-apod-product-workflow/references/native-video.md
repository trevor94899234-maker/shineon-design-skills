# Native APOD video: source, render, crop and persistence

Read only when video is required by the active task or when auditing a video failure. The thumbnail-before-video gate remains binding for the same SKU/design. Use exact accepted/eligible source files and current video authority; a thumbnail acceptance is not automatic acceptance of a new clip. Keep any specifically required human video gate.

This procedure came from 627772 C01–C04. Seven sources produced a 14-second clip in that case. **Seven, 14 seconds, four render attempts and its footer permission are observations, not new defaults or a relaxed 15-second requirement.** Verify current supported source count, output count, actual duration, controls and costs. A hard duration/count requirement must be met or remain a disclosed gap. Retain the Coach's full-build video count contract; do not substitute a storyboard or local slideshow for requested native APOD video.

## 1. Freeze the eligible set and current video state

- Record product/variant/artwork revision, accepted thumbnail manifest/hash, current selected gallery IDs/order/primary, current clip IDs/sources and save state. In a 20-image task, preserve all 20 exact final thumbnail hashes and gallery order while changing video sources.
- Record each existing clip locally when authorized/available, with hash and technical/visual/owner status. Preserve failed C01/C02/C03-style clips and reports; do not delete or relabel them to make the newest attempt appear first.
- Check generation route, remaining video attempts/cost authority, source-modification authority and human acceptance requirement in the charter. If UI reveals an unexpected charge or unsupported control, hold that action and finish independent work.
- Identify the real product-video generator/editor. A “How To Generate” button or tutorial player is not the product's video editor or evidence that the new clip exists.

## 2. Choose and bind actual sources

Select source frames for distinct useful beats and available animation margin, using the eligible same-version gallery. Prefer sources whose product corners, required copy and whole brand remain well inside the frame. A thumbnail may be valid yet unsuitable for native zoom. Do not alter the accepted main-gallery selection merely to change the video's input list unless current UI behavior requires an authorized reversible step, with a before/restore record.

For every selected video source record:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Sequence | Actual displayed source order/position, not assumed gallery order |
| Identity | Role, accepted original filename/hash/revision; derivative filename/hash if used |
| Remote source | Actual observed selected asset ID/URL, original-byte download/hash when supported, otherwise disclosed visual mapping |
| Modification | Method/ROI/source-logo authority receipt and source-to-derivative proof |
| Scene | Intended buyer beat; existing source caveats/occlusions retained |
| Animation preflight | Visible corners/logo, source margin risks and proposed supported control; no universal crop guarantee |

After any source substitution, enumerate the **actual whole list again** and update its receipt. Do not combine old seven-source proofs with only one new hash and claim all seven current sources match without checking their role/ID/hash bindings. Downloading recorded remote bytes is not a fresh browser readback by a separate reviewer.

Historical final order was **02 → 03 → 13 → 05 → 09 → 15 → 08**. Earlier source 01 and rows 07/14/16 were not C04 inputs. That order belongs to the case only; a future task needs its own observed order.

## 3. Video-only footer derivatives — only under current permission

Use original thumbnails directly unless the current task authorizes a specific derivative method. If native motion clips the bottom logo and a logo-only/footer exception is applicable:

1. Create separately named **video-only** source copies. Preserve native thumbnails and the accepted gallery manifest unchanged.
2. Use the whole original logo asset: mark, wordmark and tagline together, proportional scale, no crop/split/retype/repaint. Keep the new logo in the authorized reserved digital footer; do not modify the product/scene plane or expand the ROI over meaningful content.
3. Record original logo hash/dimensions, scale, resampling/alpha method, footer rectangle/background and exact placement. Downsample only when that is the authorized method; do not upscale to fabricate detail.
4. Compare decoded photo RGB outside the footer with the accepted source; require exact equality for this no-photo-change contract. Verify the footer against deterministic reconstruction from the actual logo asset. Keep both file hashes and the comparison result. A source-derived resized logo is not unchanged native pixels.
5. Inspect each actual derivative and whole-logo bounds. Upload/select only under current private-video authority, then bind the actual remote source. Re-hash all frozen gallery originals and recheck selection/order after the video work.

The case used 1800 × 500 → 216 × 60 logo scaling and row-specific footer ROIs. These values are not a reusable universal safe zone. Footer PASS does not pre-pass the rendered clip. Pixel identity outside a footer proves that local operation stayed within scope, not that AI product pixels match supplier artwork or physical calibration.

Without an applicable exception, prepare the exact method/ROI proposal and queue the decision; do not implement it. No image-generation or paid service call is implied by a footer proposal. A row12 artwork-inset exception does not authorize video product-plane editing.

## 4. Submit once, recover observable state

Before Generate, write an operation ID, target product, exact ordered source IDs/hashes, observed settings, intended duration/count, prior active clip and remaining allowance. Submit once through the documented UI and record its returned status/job reference.

| Observation | Required recovery |
|---|---|
| Busy/pending job | Observe that same job/product within supported waits; keep the submission counted |
| Tool timeout, detached element or unknown result after Generate | Inspect generator status, video cards, source URL and timestamps before any new click; do not generate a duplicate |
| Video appears but download times out | Retrieve the same observed clip through supported controls; a retrieval retry is not a new render |
| Save times out | Reload/reopen and inspect actual persisted source and state; do not assume failure or blindly save again |
| Confirmed terminal render failure | Preserve evidence; correct only the identified cause within remaining explicit allowance |
| Safety stop or login/challenge | Follow the current supported stop/handoff rules; no controller/runtime switch to evade it |

Unknown is a state, not a failed submission that can be erased from the ledger. If current job state cannot be established, hold another render and report the precise unresolved operation. Keep other authorized work moving.

## 5. Inspect the actual clip, not just its sources

Download the actual generated clip through the normal supported route and bind filename, bytes, SHA-256, observed remote source and render operation. Use available local media tools for:

- Codec/container, width/height, duration, frame rate/time base, frame count when reliable, and presence/absence of audio. Report measured duration, not the target rounded upward.
- Decode the complete file to detect corrupt/truncated frames. Record the executable/method/result and whether this agent ran it or only read another agent's receipt.
- Extract inspection frames from that **same hash** with timestamps/index convention. Keep source-to-segment mapping based on actual output; encoding is lossy, so MP4 frames do not have PNG byte identity.

For **every actual segment**, inspect the first visible/start frame, middle, late near maximum zoom, last visible/end frame, and both sides of each transition. Check the final endpoint too. If fades begin in black, inspect the first sufficiently visible frame as well as the boundary; black/fade is not itself a product defect, but cannot hide a confirmed crop before fade. If risk is near the edge, inspect the surrounding contiguous native frames rather than relying only on a contact sheet.

Record exact timestamps and frame indices. For a constant-rate clip with one-based filenames, time is `(index - 1) / fps`; variable-rate footage needs actual timestamps. A full decode or extraction of 350 frames is **not** a claim of visually inspecting all 350. State `full decode` and `targeted sampled visual QA` separately, listing the frames actually viewed.

Inspect independently:

1. Full product corners/required silhouette and printed art during motion; distinguish original accepted hand/knee occlusion from **new frame crop**.
2. The **whole** brand unit throughout zoom, including lower mark/wordmark/tagline bounds. Entirely in-frame does not mean tiny tagline text is individually readable.
3. Correct source/segment order, intended useful roles, normal-light copy readability, transitions, scaling, distortion and unexpected content/audio.
4. Existing AI illustration/source limitations. Photo motion is not real customer moving footage; no footage/testimonial claim is created by rendering it.

One verified crop prevents whole-clip visual PASS even if the other six segments and decode pass. Source thumbnail PASS, estimated crop boxes and a good middle frame cannot substitute for late/end checks. Native crop/zoom can change across settings or versions; do not invent a permanent pixel margin or renderer crop function.

## 6. Correct the failed layer within authority

| Actual failure | Bounded response |
|---|---|
| Logo clipped, product intact | Consider a current-authorized footer derivative or supported native framing control; validate the actual next clip |
| Product corner clipped during zoom | Choose a better-margin eligible source or supported native framing/motion setting; do not paint corners, warp the mat or rewrite accepted gallery art |
| One segment fails late after others were repaired | Preserve prior findings and test the complete next clip; do not accept based only on the replaced segments |
| Extra source/control/method/call needed outside authority | Queue exact proposal, incremental cap/cost and current clip preview; no silent extra render |
| Owner accepts an identified clip despite a disclosed allowable defect | Bind exact clip hash/purpose/limits and retain failed technical findings; do not inherit that exception for another clip |

A suggested source central crop can help shortlist inputs but is a diagnostic hypothesis only. In C03, row01 looked acceptable at middle/early-late frames yet cropped at its late end. Replacing it with row08 fixed the observed C04 samples; this does not prove a universally safe row08 or renderer.

## 7. Save, reload and prove the actual player

Use the real current Save/attach action for the identified product. Reopen/reload after completion and bind the displayed clip's source/asset to the inspected file or disclosed transformed equivalent. Play the **actual saved product player**, observe that playback starts and reaches the actual end, and record source, duration, final time and ended state when exposed. A poster frame is not playback proof.

Capture a product-player view with a recognizably matching scene. Do not accept a filename such as `VIDEO-SAVED.png` as proof: the 627772 case had a similarly named screenshot of an APOD “How To” tutorial with an unrelated cup. That tutorial was explicitly excluded. A later paused preview and earlier ended-state observation are different moments; identify both rather than claiming one screenshot proves everything.

After the **last** video save, reread the full title, complete description, actual distinct tags, required personalization/instructions, live-preview state, exact variant/SKU/artwork, unchanged price/excluded settings, AutoFulfill and gallery count/order/primary. Keep the official 20 thumbnails unchanged when the video-only method requires that. Obtain exact-clip owner acceptance only when the charter requires it; a QA agent does not grant it.

Deliver the exact clip and [video record](../assets/video-record.md), with separate technical, sampled visual, source-binding, persisted-player and human-gate statuses. Latest named acceptance/completion can supersede historical pending status without editing old receipts. No local QA result alone proves APOD persistence or Etsy publication.
