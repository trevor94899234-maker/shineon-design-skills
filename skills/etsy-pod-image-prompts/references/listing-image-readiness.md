# Listing-image readiness and provenance

Checked against Etsy's [Listing Image Requirements](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005/) on 9 October 2026; the page is marked updated 9 July 2024. Recheck the official source before current publication advice.

## Identify the role before deciding readiness

Record product/SKU, buyer angle, intended slot/use, final file hash, source type and source evidence. Keep a hero-style marketing preview separate from a verified first listing photograph.

For personalized/customized goods, Etsy requires the first image to show an actual finished customized item comparable to the buyer's order. A blank base, placeholder-text mockup or generated photorealistic scene does not establish this requirement. Additional generated mockups can illustrate customization. Buyer-personalized POD goods remain subject to the personalized-image rule.

Ask for missing source provenance only when it changes the intended use. Prefer original sample photography and attributable production records; neither a filename/photo label nor the absence of EXIF establishes whether the product was physically photographed. Do not infer that an old file is unlawful merely because its provenance is unknown.

## Separate the gates

Record each independently: source/rights verified; product and brand fidelity; physical integration; buyer-purpose/thumbnail readability; owner acceptance of the exact version; intended-slot policy readiness; production proof; publication.

An attractive, source-faithful composite can pass visual QA and be delivered for review while first-image readiness stays HOLD. Owner approval of its appearance does not prove photography provenance or authorize publication. Preserve historical approvals with their actual files and scope; reopen only affected checks after a revision.

Keep generated gifting scenes clearly illustrative and do not claim customer endorsement. Preserve the verified original MG A5 asset where required; branding and a disclosure footer cannot cure a false product or source claim.

Deliver actual files with intended-use labels and unresolved gates. Do not count prepared files, accepted message sends, owner downloads, owner approvals, production acceptance and live publication as one completed state.
