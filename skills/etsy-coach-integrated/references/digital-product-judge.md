# Digital Product judge

Version 2026.10.13.2

Use this owner-requested quality gate for new or materially revised digital products before preparing a release. Apply it to the actual buyer files: printables, workbooks, journals, learning cards, templates, and similar downloads. Physical POD and ornaments remain on their own product/mockup workflow. Do not silently transfer digital findings, page counts, or simulated responses to physical listings.

## 1 Freeze the actual input

- Fix the product purpose, intended buyer/user, age or ability scope, language, file formats, and authorized revision scope.
- Save a read-only snapshot of the current source content, every complete buyer PDF/template, actual rendered pages, and a file manifest with hashes and page counts. Never overwrite a previous round.
- Build stable identifiers for every user-answer prompt, activity, card, and source page. Split composite tasks in the record so each subquestion is addressed. Static headings, licenses, and metadata need page-level review; do not count them as extra answer questions.
- Record exactly which pages and formats exist. Text-only review and visual review are different evidence. A two-page proof cannot establish that a complete ten-page product works.
- For Scripture products, lock the authorized Bible edition and exact quoted text. Keep original explanations, word help, prayer starters, and examples distinct from quotations. Do not modernize quoted KJV words, spelling, capitalization, or punctuation.

## 2 Use ten independent hypothetical reader perspectives

- Create ten distinct adult personas with relevant variation in life context, age, gender, English reading comfort, writing confidence, domain familiarity, and available time. For Christian products vary faith duration and KJV familiarity without treating either as spiritual quality.
- State that every persona, attempt, and reaction is simulated. Never call them customer interviews, recruited participants, real testimonials, child tests, clinical evidence, or measured effectiveness.
- Use a fresh context for each persona. Pass only the current frozen artifacts, assigned persona, neutral task, and output schema. Do not expose earlier verdicts, desired fixes, or another persona's response before that independent submission is frozen.
- Honor a user-specified model and effort. If capacity is limited, run the ten independent reviews sequentially or in batches. Do not replace ten independent passes with one writer inventing a unanimous panel or imply concurrency that did not occur.
- Preserve individual agency and disagreements. Do not assume demographic preferences or impairments. Do not force a positive emotional reaction or a complaint.
- Keep the same product scope for all ten perspectives. When direct child/family use is inapplicable, still inspect each relevant prompt honestly as an adult buyer, recipient, or helper; mark child enactment as not applicable. Never invent access to a child or claim an imagined child's success.

## 3 Actually walk through every item and page

For every answer prompt or card, record:

1. Persona and stable item/page ID
2. Applicability and role
3. A concrete attempted answer, action, preparation choice, or honest decision to skip; address each component of a composite task
4. A specific sticking point, or an explicit no-issue result
5. The smallest assistance wanted, if any
6. Whether an optional cue helps or leads the answer
7. Retain/change recommendation and priority
8. Evidence basis: source text, actual rendered page, or unverified proposed layout

Inspect every complete rendered page in the assigned format at a readable scale. Record a brief page-level observation and the exact image/page inspected. Distribute supported formats across reviewers and separately verify every exported format; state that a reader who inspected Letter did not personally inspect A4. Do not infer physical print quality from a screen render.

Evaluate the actual purpose and usage: clear starting point, meaningful prompts, understandable vocabulary, practical instructions, response space, accessibility choices, visual hierarchy, accurate contents/printing promises, and safe age-appropriate boundaries. Optional examples should prompt the reader's own thinking, not prescribe faith, gratitude, a polished prayer, or a required feeling. Keep privacy, refusal, rest, questions, and partial completion compatible with the product's purpose.

## 4 Freeze the report before revising

- Validate ten complete submissions against the item/page manifest. Identify missing, duplicate, empty, and inapplicable records. Coverage counts are not success rates.
- Synthesize concrete findings with source IDs and persona evidence. Separate observed defects, plausible usability friction, individual preferences, and unresolved questions.
- Rank release blockers first, then repeated or high-impact friction, then optional polish. Preserve successful parts. Do not manufacture changes to make a round appear productive.
- Include exact current wording and the smallest recommended wording/layout change for adopted findings. Keep disagreements visible; do not force a majority vote or remove a useful option because one persona dislikes it.
- Freeze the aggregate report, full structured evidence, coverage audit, and proposed change list before product edits. If the user requested the report first, send it before implementation. Otherwise preserve the report as the pre-revision checkpoint within the authorized workflow.

## 5 Revise and run final QA

- Apply only authorized, evidence-backed changes to a new version. Keep a field-level before/after change log and rejected/deferred suggestions with reasons.
- Preserve content counts, intended audience, product purpose, Scripture locks, and safety boundaries unless the user approves a scope change. Reconcile every changed page/card count across files, guides, covers, previews, and planned listing claims.
- Render all complete buyer files. Inspect every page for clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, tiny text, response space, print margins, cutting boundaries, links, and image relevance. Compare extracted quotations with the locked source.
- Retest the original friction points on the revised artifacts. This is a bounded regression check, not a fabricated second customer study.
- Verify delivered files can be opened and match the reviewed versions using [digital delivery integrity](digital-delivery-integrity.md). Keep byte identity, rendered/content parity and accepted-send evidence separate; a transport metadata change is not automatically changed buyer content. Describe remaining limits, including actual printing, genuine customer use, or legal/rights clearance. Do not claim cloud, dashboard, Vault, or marketplace persistence without readback evidence for that destination.

## 6 Requested repeated loops

Use the user's explicitly requested cycle count and product scope. A request for five full cycles means five completed frozen design → ten independent reviews → frozen report → justified revision sequences. Do not count an unreviewed baseline as one cycle or substitute four additional cycles for five full cycles. If the user expands the product scope, preserve completed evidence, record the scope change, and complete every required product in every requested cycle before claiming full completion.

For a multi-round request, each round must independently review the preceding frozen revised artifact, then freeze its report, then implement justified changes, then produce and verify the next snapshot. Use fresh contexts and do not combine several purported rounds into one retrospective report. Preserve earlier round evidence. A clean round may retain a product unchanged, with a reason; it must still have the required review coverage.

Track the review-cycle number separately from each product's technical version. Products joining with different technical baselines may be reviewed in frozen waves, but a cycle is complete only when its entire stated product scope has passed the required review and revision gates. Map user-facing editions to technical file versions before starting. Report completion only when the requested final edition and its QA are actually ready. Give a concise final account of changes, retained strengths, unresolved issues, and the limits of simulation, plus the actual buyer files and review record.

## Minimal record contract

A round contains input_manifest, ten independent reviewer submissions, coverage_audit, frozen_findings, change_spec, implemented_change_log, output_manifest, and final_qa. A reviewer submission contains profile, item_records, page_observations, inspected_pages, top_findings, and limitations. Keep these as user-owned task artifacts, with clear simulation labels and no private working thoughts.

An example digital journal with 24 prompts, a family workbook with 24 prompts, and 12 cards plus 2 review prompts requires 62 item records per persona and 620 across ten. Recompute counts from the actual current manifest; do not hard-code these example counts for later products.
